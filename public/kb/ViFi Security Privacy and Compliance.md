# ViFi Security, Privacy and Compliance

## Security basics
Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Accounts support Google sign-in and two-factor authentication with an authenticator app, text message, or email, with backup codes for a lost phone. Alerts for sign-ins from new devices and for changes to two-factor settings cannot be turned off. Access inside a workspace is controlled by roles: Admin, Member, and Viewer. Recording playback is limited to roles with recording access, and every playback, download, and deletion is logged in an immutable audit log.

## What is stored for each call
The caller's phone number and the time, duration, and outcome of the call; the transcript, summary, action items, tags, and caller facts; the recording, if recording is on, until the retention period ends; and any text messages sent or received on the ViFi number. ViFi builds a profile per phone number from what callers say; the team can edit or remove facts, and a profile can be deleted on request.

## Who can see what
The business's team, according to their roles. ViFi staff, only when needed to support the business or keep the service running, under confidentiality obligations. Connected tools receive only what the enabled automations send them, such as a summary posted to Slack. ViFi does not sell call or caller data.

## Recording and consent
Recording is on by default for new workspaces, with a retention period that defaults to 90 days and an editable recording notice the agent reads at the start of each call. Some US states require every party to consent to recording; the notice at the start of the call is the usual way to get it, and a caller who objects can hang up before saying anything substantive. ViFi cannot give legal advice on which rule applies; businesses that take payment details over the phone may prefer to turn recording off.

## Deleting data
A recording can be deleted from the call page and the deletion is logged. A caller's data can be deleted by emailing support@vifi.us with the phone number. A whole workspace and everything in it can be deleted at an Admin's request. A person can delete their own account from the Account page. A self-serve data export is planned; until then support prepares exports on request.

## Where data lives
ViFi runs on cloud infrastructure in the United States. Calls are processed by speech and language providers under contract to ViFi, as listed in the privacy policy at vifi.us slash legal slash privacy. The terms of service are at vifi.us slash legal slash terms.

## HIPAA and regulated industries
ViFi is not currently offered as a HIPAA-covered service with a business associate agreement. Medical and dental offices use ViFi for scheduling and callback intake, and the agent never gives medical advice. Law firms use it for intake, and the agent never gives legal advice. Businesses with specific compliance needs should email hello@vifi.us before relying on ViFi for regulated conversations.
