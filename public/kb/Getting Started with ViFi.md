# Getting Started with ViFi

## What you need
An email address, about ten minutes, and your own mobile phone so you can call your agent and hear it answer. A business phone number you can forward helps, but you can also hand out the ViFi number directly. No card is needed for the free trial, and there is nothing to install.

## Creating an account
Go to app.vifi.us slash register. Enter your email and a password of at least ten characters, or click Sign in with Google. Verify your email from the message ViFi sends. Then give your business name; that creates your workspace and makes you its Admin. If a colleague invited you, use the link in the invitation email instead, and the workspace appears in your account.

## The Setup Guide: four steps
Step one, find your business: start typing the business name and pick it from Google's listings; ViFi reads the website and listing to learn what you do, your services, and your hours. Step two, get your number: ViFi suggests an area code and shows available local numbers to pick from. Step three, meet your agent: review the drafted business profile and hours, choose the agent's name, voice, and greeting, and switch on capabilities such as taking messages. Step four, call your agent: call the number from your own phone, which is already approved, and watch the summary and transcript appear. The guide completes on its own when the first call finishes.

## Forwarding your calls
You keep your existing number. To let ViFi answer, dial a forwarding code on the phone whose calls you want forwarded, or use your phone system's forwarding setting. Overflow forwarding, which sends only unanswered and busy calls, is the most common choice; forwarding everything is common at night. The help center at docs.vifi.us lists the codes for AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, and most landline and VoIP services, and how to turn forwarding off. Codes vary by carrier, and carriers will set it up on request.

## Testing during the trial
During the trial only up to three approved phone numbers reach the agent; your own mobile is approved automatically, and you can add two more from the Callers page. You can also call your agent from your web browser on the Agent page. Ask your three most common customer questions, ask something it should not answer, ask for a person, and say goodbye, then fix whatever was off in the greeting, knowledge base, policies, or transfers.

## Your first week
Read the briefing from each early call and check the summary matched what was said. Open Unanswered in the knowledge base and convert real questions into articles. Write three to five plain-English policies. Add tricky names to the pronunciation guide. Set your business hours if the guide did not get them right. Decide who gets notified and how. Connect a calendar or CRM. Set a spending alert. Subscribe before the trial ends so your number keeps answering and transfers switch on.
