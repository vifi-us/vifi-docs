# ViFi Plans and Pricing

## The three plans
ViFi is a monthly subscription with three plans. Starter is $49 a month and includes 100 AI minutes and 100 text segments. Growth is $149 a month and includes 500 AI minutes and 500 text segments; it is the most popular plan. Business is $349 a month and includes 2,000 AI minutes and 2,000 text segments. Every plan includes the full product: summaries and transcripts, recordings, caller memory, the knowledge base and menu, integrations and automations, the website widget, and unlimited teammates. The plans differ only in included usage and the price of extra usage. Current prices are on vifi.us slash pricing and in the checkout.

## Extra usage rates
When a business uses more than its included minutes, extra minutes are charged at the plan rate: 15 cents per minute on Starter, 12 cents on Growth, and 10 cents on Business. Extra text segments are 3 cents each on Starter and Growth and 2 cents on Business. Extra usage is billed at the end of the period it happened in. The dashboard shows usage in near real time, and the business can set a spending alert and a hard stop limit for extra usage.

## What an AI minute is
An AI minute is one minute of the agent's time on a call, rounded up to the next whole minute per call. A call lasting two minutes and ten seconds counts as three AI minutes. Browser test calls and website widget conversations count the same way. Time after a transfer, when the caller is talking to the business's own team, does not count. Calls that hang up before a conversation use almost nothing, and blocked numbers use no minutes because they are rejected before the agent answers.

## What a text segment is
Carriers split text messages into segments of about 160 characters, so a long text is two or three segments. Segments sent and received on the business's ViFi number both count toward the plan's included segments. Texts ViFi sends the owner personally, such as sign-in codes and call summaries, do not count.

## The free trial
Every plan starts with a seven-day free trial with no credit card. The trial includes 25 AI minutes and 50 text segments and everything in the product. During the trial only up to three approved phone numbers can reach the agent, so the business can test without strangers using trial minutes, and transfers to a person are off; the agent takes a message instead. Both switch on the moment the business subscribes. A business can subscribe at any point during the trial; the card is not charged until the trial ends, and the plan starts automatically on that date.

## When the trial ends without a subscription
The business can still sign in, read calls, and change settings. Callers to the ViFi number hear a short message that the assistant is temporarily unavailable. Knowledge base uploads, new phone numbers, and texts pause. Subscribing from the Billing page brings everything back within a minute, with the number and settings kept.

## Paying, invoices, and promo codes
Payment is by card through a secure Stripe checkout. The monthly fee is charged at the start of each period; extra usage at the end. The checkout has a field for a promotion code. Invoices are listed in the dashboard and can be downloaded as PDFs, showing the plan fee and extra usage separately. If a payment fails, ViFi emails the owner, Stripe retries the card for several days, and the agent keeps answering during that time; if every retry fails the subscription pauses until a payment succeeds.

## Changing or cancelling
Upgrades are immediate from the dashboard and prorated. Moving to a smaller plan is handled by support at support@vifi.us and takes effect at the end of the billing period; self-serve downgrades are coming soon. Cancelling is done from the billing portal, and service continues to the end of the paid period. There are no contracts, no cancellation fees, and no setup fees. Settings, knowledge base, and call history are kept if the business comes back.

## Choosing a plan
Estimate calls per month and multiply by about three minutes. Fifty calls a month is roughly 150 minutes, which fits Growth comfortably or Starter with some extra-minute charges. Plans can be changed later. For needs beyond the Business plan, email hello@vifi.us.
