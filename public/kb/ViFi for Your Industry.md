# ViFi for Your Industry

## Home services and contractors
Plumbers, electricians, HVAC technicians, roofers, and contractors miss calls on the job site, and every missed call is a job that may go to a competitor. ViFi answers when the crew cannot, captures who called, where they are, what service they need, and when, and sends a briefing so the callback starts with context instead of guesswork. It follows a written after-hours policy, collects the request, and flags situations that need a person. Typical setup: overflow forwarding during the day, forward everything after hours, transfer destination set to the owner or dispatcher.

## HVAC companies
When temperatures spike, several callers try to get through while the office helps one, and technicians in an attic cannot stop for every new lead. ViFi covers overflow and after-hours calls, collects the property, system type, symptoms, and preferred timing without attempting a diagnosis, and briefs a dispatcher, owner, or technician for the next step. Safety concerns are captured and flagged for human review rather than answered.

## Plumbing companies
An active leak and a future remodel sound alike in a voicemail. ViFi asks what the caller can see and when it started, records the address, the affected fixture or area, and any safety concern, and delivers a briefing so urgent jobs get prioritized and estimates get the details the estimator needs. Diagnosis, safety advice, and pricing decisions stay with qualified people unless the business has defined a safe answer.

## Electricians
Electricians at a panel or on a ladder cannot safely stop for every call, and some callers describe sparks, odors, or outages that need a clear escalation policy rather than improvised advice. ViFi answers within the safety wording the business approves, collects the location, symptoms, project details, and timing, avoids remote troubleshooting, and sends a complete briefing for the office or on-call electrician. Estimate requests for EV chargers, panel upgrades, lighting, and generators arrive with the details the estimator wants.

## Medical and dental offices
Front desks juggle check-ins, scheduling, and phones at once, so patients get busy signals, and after-hours calls disappear into voicemail. ViFi handles overflow and after-hours calls, captures what the patient needs and their callback details, and gives staff a reliable record of the conversation. It does not give medical advice. ViFi is not currently offered as a HIPAA-covered service with a business associate agreement; offices that need one should email hello@vifi.us.

## Law firms
One missed intake call can mean thousands in lost revenue, and most prospective clients will not leave a voicemail. ViFi answers every intake call with a professional voice, captures the caller's situation and contact details with the nuance a generic answering service misses, and delivers a transcript and recording so attorneys can decide which calls to return first. It never gives legal advice.

## Real estate agents
Leads call during showings and open houses, and the first agent to respond usually wins. ViFi answers, talks with the prospect, and sends an instant heads-up with what they want, so the agent can prioritize hot leads and respond first. Caller memory keeps the details of dozens of active leads straight across calls.

## Restaurants, salons, and shops
Any business that gets calls about hours, prices, availability, or booking is a fit. Business hours answer the most common question, the menu feature holds items and prices, the knowledge base answers policy questions, and a connected calendar or Square Appointments lets the agent book. Multilingual mode helps businesses with Spanish-speaking customers.
