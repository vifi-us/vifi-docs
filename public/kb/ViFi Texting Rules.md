# ViFi Texting Rules

## Why texting from a business number is limited
US carriers require any business that texts customers from a regular ten-digit number to be registered, with an approved description of what it sends and how customers agree to it. Messages outside the approved program are filtered or blocked, and violations can get a number shut off. ViFi is registered as one program covering every business on the platform, which keeps businesses out of the paperwork but means every text has to fit what is approved.

## What works today
Reading messages: anyone who texts a business's ViFi number appears as a conversation in the Messages page, with an all-messages view for checking a given day. The one-time follow-up text: on a call, if the caller asks for something in writing, the agent can offer ViFi's one-time text, read a short required notice, and if the caller clearly agrees, send one fixed confirmation message. STOP, START, and HELP keywords are handled automatically and cannot be changed.

## What does not work yet
Replying to texts from the dashboard, custom texts composed by the agent, sending links, texting a summary to the caller, and automatic missed-call texts are all coming soon, waiting on carrier approval of an expanded program. These are limited by registration rules, not by a setting the business can change.

## Texts to the business owner
Texts ViFi sends the owner, such as call summaries after each call, sign-in codes, and billing alerts, come from ViFi's own toll-free number under a separate verified program and work normally. The owner agrees to them when verifying a mobile number, and replying STOP stops them.

## Usage
Text segments sent and received on the business's ViFi number both count toward the plan's included segments, then bill at the plan's per-segment rate.
