# ViFi Support and Company

## Getting help
Email support@vifi.us for help with an account. A person reads every message and most replies go out within one business day. Include the workspace name, a link to the call if it is about a call, what was expected and what happened, and a screenshot of any error. For questions before signing up, email hello@vifi.us. The help center at docs.vifi.us has step-by-step guides for setup, every feature, troubleshooting, and best practices, plus a Feature status page and a What's new page.

## What support can do that the dashboard cannot
Remove two-factor authentication after verifying identity. Move an account to a smaller plan. Delete a workspace, a caller's data, or an account and its data. Ban an abusive caller across the whole platform. Investigate a call that failed before it reached the workspace. Prepare a data export.

## About ViFi
ViFi is a US company that builds an AI phone assistant for small businesses. The product is live, the website is vifi.us, the application is app.vifi.us, and the help center is docs.vifi.us. ViFi's own phone line is answered by ViFi: callers who reach the company are talking to the same product ViFi sells, using the knowledge base, menu, transfers, and briefings that customers get.

## Talking to a person at ViFi
Callers who want a person can ask, and the agent will take a message or connect them if someone is available. Sales conversations, custom plans, and partnership questions go to hello@vifi.us.
