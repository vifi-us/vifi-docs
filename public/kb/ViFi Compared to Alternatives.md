# ViFi Compared to Alternatives

## ViFi versus voicemail
Voicemail records a message for later; most callers do not leave one, and the ones who do leave vague messages the business has to decode. ViFi has a two-way conversation, asks follow-up questions, answers from the business's approved information, and delivers a summary, action items, and a full transcript, so the callback starts with context. Recording is optional in ViFi; voicemail only has the message itself.

## ViFi versus a human answering service
Live operators can hold a conversation, but their notes depend on their training and rarely include a transcript. ViFi answers every call instantly with no hold time, uses only the business's approved information, remembers repeat callers, and includes the summary and transcript with every call. Businesses that need a person for some calls set a transfer destination so the agent hands those callers to a human.

## ViFi versus other AI receptionists
Many AI phone products stop at answering, and their follow-up questions, use of business information, and post-call summaries vary by provider. ViFi is built around what happens after the call: every conversation produces a summary, action items, a word-for-word transcript, and an optional recording, and callers are recognized on their next call. It also refuses to invent prices, hours, or policies and tells the business what callers asked that it could not answer.

## ViFi versus hiring a receptionist
A receptionist works set hours and costs a salary. ViFi answers around the clock, costs $49 to $349 a month depending on volume, and never forgets to write down the callback number. It is not a replacement for a person who handles complex service; it is the reason that person starts every callback prepared.

## What ViFi will not do
It does not place outbound calls, does not send free-form text messages yet, does not give medical, legal, or financial advice, and does not answer questions it has not been given information for; in that case it takes a message or offers a transfer.
