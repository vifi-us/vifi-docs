# ViFi Overview and Features

## What ViFi is
ViFi is an AI phone assistant for small businesses. It answers calls in a natural voice, helps callers the way a good front-desk person would, and sends the business a written recap of every conversation: a summary, action items, the full transcript, and a recording when recording is on. Businesses keep their existing phone number and forward calls to ViFi, or hand out the ViFi number directly. There is nothing to install. Setup takes about ten minutes and starts with a free seven-day trial that needs no credit card.

## What happens on a call
The call reaches the ViFi number, directly or by forwarding. The agent answers with the greeting the business wrote, in the voice they chose, using the name they gave it. It answers questions about the business and its hours, looks up answers in the knowledge base or menu, takes messages, books appointments through a connected calendar, or transfers the caller to a real person. When the caller is done, it wraps up. Within a minute or two the business has the summary, action items, transcript, and recording, delivered by email, text, Slack, Discord, or into HubSpot.

## What the agent knows
The agent only knows what the business tells it. It draws on the business profile (name, description, services, hours, industry), the knowledge base (short question-and-answer articles, uploaded documents, and web pages), the menu or services list with prices, plain-English policies such as never quoting prices, and connected tools such as a calendar or CRM. It never invents policies, prices, or availability. When it does not know, it takes a message or offers a transfer, and the question is listed in the dashboard as unanswered so the owner can add the answer.

## After the call: briefings
Every call produces a briefing. The summary says who called, what they wanted, and how it ended. Action items are things the team needs to do, such as calling someone back about a quote. Tags label the topic and outcome. Caller facts are details worth remembering, such as an address or a preferred time, saved to the caller's profile. Unanswered questions list anything the agent could not answer. The transcript is word for word. The recording, if on, plays from a private link and every play is logged. Briefings arrive within one to two minutes.

## Caller memory
ViFi keeps a profile for every phone number that calls, built automatically from the calls: whether they are a first-time or returning caller, how their calls have felt over time, facts they mentioned, open follow-ups, notes the team adds, and a timeline of every call. The agent reads the profile at the start of each call, so returning callers are recognized and the agent uses what it already knows.

## Voice, language, and personality
The business chooses a voice from a catalog with a recommended set, previews each one, names the agent, and describes its tone in a few words such as warm, professional, concise. The agent speaks English by default and can be set to Dutch, French, German, Hindi, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese, Russian, or Spanish, and it can switch to the caller's language mid-call. A pronunciation guide teaches it names and product words it would otherwise say wrong.

## Policies and capabilities
Policies are plain-English rules the agent follows on every call, such as never discuss pricing, always offer to transfer refund requests, or never promise an appointment without checking the calendar. Policies beat everything else. Capabilities are switches for what the agent may do: check business hours, search the knowledge base, look up the menu, take a message, transfer to a human, send the one-time follow-up text, and end the call. Connected tools add more, such as booking.

## Transfers to a person
On a paid plan, the business sets one or more transfer destinations in order, each with a phone number and a description of when to route there, for example billing questions or anyone asking for Maria. The agent can announce the caller first or transfer straight through. If nobody answers, it takes a message. During the free trial, transfers are off and the agent takes a message instead.

## Notifications
After every call the business can get an email with the summary and transcript, a text with a short recap and a link, or a daily summary email. Recipients do not need a ViFi account. Missed-call alerts, Slack and Discord posts, and CRM logging are automations. Account alerts, such as a trial ending or a new sign-in, go to the owner by email or text.

## Blocking spam and abuse
Numbers can be blocked by hand with a private reason, or ViFi can block likely spam and rapid repeat callers automatically; every automatic block is labeled and can be undone. Blocked calls are rejected before the agent answers, so they use no minutes and never appear as real conversations. A list of recently rejected calls makes it easy to check nothing legitimate was caught.

## Analytics
The analytics page shows total calls, average duration, answered versus missed by day, how callers felt, what they called about, and agent performance such as transfers and bookings. It is in beta while a few measures, such as first-call resolution, are added.

## Website widget
A voice widget puts a talk-to-us button on the business website. Visitors click it and talk to the same agent from their browser, trained on the same knowledge. It is added with one line of code, the launcher style and text are configurable, and the business controls which websites may use it. Conversations appear in call history like phone calls and use AI minutes the same way.

## Team and security
Teammates are invited as Admins, Members, or Viewers. Admins manage billing and configuration, Members keep the knowledge base current and read calls, Viewers only read. Every change is recorded in an immutable audit log. Accounts support Google sign-in, two-factor authentication with an authenticator app, text, or email, backup codes, and sign-in alerts that cannot be turned off.

## What is not available yet
Free-form text messages and links to callers, replying to texts from the dashboard, and automatic missed-call texts are coming soon, waiting on carrier approval of an expanded messaging program. Sign in with Apple and Microsoft, passkeys, self-serve plan downgrades, a data export, and connecting your own systems through MCP servers are planned. Outbound calling is not offered. The current list is at docs.vifi.us under Feature status.
