## What it does

- **Email call summary to team.** After the summary is ready, sends it to addresses you list, from your Gmail account.
- **Send follow-up email to caller.** If the caller gave an email address on the call, sends them a short follow-up based on the conversation.

Both run after the summary is ready.

## Connect

1. Open **Integrations** and click **Connect** on Gmail.
2. Choose the Google account to send from and approve access. Use a shared account such as `office@` if you don't want mail sent as you personally.

## Turn on the automations

1. Open **Automations** and find **After a call ends**.
2. Switch on **Email call summary to team** and enter the recipients.
3. Switch on **Send follow-up email to caller** if you want callers emailed.

::screenshot[The Gmail automation cards with the recipient list]

## Gmail or built-in email?

ViFi can email transcripts to your team without Gmail at all; see [Notifications](/calls-and-callers/notifications/). Use the Gmail integration when you want mail to come from your own address, land in your Sent folder, or go to callers.
