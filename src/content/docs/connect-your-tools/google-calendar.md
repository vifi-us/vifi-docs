## What it does

While on a call, the agent can:

- Check availability in a date range.
- Book an appointment.
- Look up an existing appointment.
- Change or cancel one.

Each is a separate automation, so you can allow booking but not cancelling, for example.

## Connect

1. Open **Integrations** and click **Connect** on Google Calendar.
2. Choose the Google account whose calendar you want to use.
3. Approve access.

## Turn on booking

1. Open **Automations** and find the **While your agent is on a call** section.
2. Switch on **Check Google Calendar availability**.
3. Switch on the actions you want: **Book**, **Look up**, **Modify**, **Cancel**.
4. When asked, choose which calendar to use. Save.

## How a booking call goes

The agent asks what the caller wants, checks availability, offers times, confirms the caller's name and phone number, and books. The caller's details go into the event, and the call briefing records the booking.

## Tips

- Set your [business hours](/your-agent/business-profile/) so the agent doesn't offer times when you're closed.
- Add a [policy](/your-agent/call-behavior/) such as "Appointments are 60 minutes; don't book back to back" if your calendar doesn't encode that.
- Keep a dedicated "ViFi bookings" calendar if you want to review bookings before they hit your main schedule.
