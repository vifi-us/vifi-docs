## What it does

With Square Appointments connected, the agent can check availability and book, look up, change, or cancel bookings while the caller is on the line. Each action is its own automation.

## Connect

1. Open **Integrations** and click **Connect** on Square.
2. Sign in to Square and approve access.

## Turn on the actions

1. Open **Automations** and find **While your agent is on a call**.
2. Switch on **Check Square availability**, then the actions you want to allow: book, look up, modify, cancel.
3. Choose the location and services when asked. Save.

::screenshot[The Square automation cards]

## Tips

- Keep your service list in Square current; the agent offers what Square offers.
- If you also keep a [menu](/your-agent/menu-and-services/) in ViFi for prices, make sure the names match Square's, so the agent doesn't describe one and book another.
