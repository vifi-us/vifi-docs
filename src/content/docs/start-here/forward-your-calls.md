You keep your existing business number. To let ViFi answer, you either forward calls from that number to your ViFi number, or give out the ViFi number directly. Your ViFi number is on **Channels** in the sidebar.

## Pick a forwarding style

| Style | When ViFi answers | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Overflow** (recommended) | Only when you don't pick up, or the line is busy | Businesses that still want to answer when they can |
| **Everything** | Every call, immediately | After hours, holidays, or when you never want to pick up |
| **Direct** | Calls made to the ViFi number itself | Ads, websites, or a dedicated "book an appointment" line |

You can switch styles whenever you like. Many businesses use overflow during the day and everything at night.

## Dial codes by carrier

Dial the code from the phone whose calls you want to forward. Replace `NUMBER` with your ten-digit ViFi number.

:::caution
Codes vary by carrier and plan, and some office phone systems use their own settings. If a code doesn't work, search your carrier's help site for "call forwarding" or ask them to set it up. They can usually do it in a minute.
:::

### AT&T (mobile)

| Forward | Turn on | Turn off |
|---|---|---|
| Everything | `**21*NUMBER#` | `##21#` |
| When unanswered | `**61*NUMBER#` | `##61#` |
| When busy | `**67*NUMBER#` | `##67#` |

### Verizon (mobile)

| Forward | Turn on | Turn off |
|---|---|---|
| Everything | `*72NUMBER` | `*73` |
| When busy or unanswered | `*71NUMBER` | `*73` |

### T-Mobile (mobile)

| Forward | Turn on | Turn off |
|---|---|---|
| Everything | `**21*NUMBER#` | `##21#` |
| When unanswered | `**61*NUMBER#` | `##61#` |
| When busy | `**67*NUMBER#` | `##67#` |

### Landlines and most VoIP lines

| Forward | Turn on | Turn off |
|---|---|---|
| Everything | `*72` then `NUMBER` | `*73` |
| When unanswered | `*92` then `NUMBER` | `*93` |
| When busy | `*90` then `NUMBER` | `*91` |

Listen for a confirmation tone or an announcement after dialling. Some lines ring the destination once to confirm; let it ring through.

### Google Voice, RingCentral, and other app-based services

These usually have a call-forwarding or "ring my other numbers" setting in the app or web settings rather than a dial code. Look under **Settings**, then **Calls**. Add your ViFi number as the destination.

## Test it

1. Call your business number from a phone that isn't the one being forwarded.
2. For overflow forwarding, let it ring without answering.
3. Your agent should pick up with your greeting. If it doesn't, see [Calls aren't reaching ViFi](/fix-a-problem/calls-not-reaching-vifi/).

:::tip
During the free trial, ViFi only answers [approved callers](/calls-and-callers/approved-callers/). Test from an approved phone, or approve the phone you're testing with.
:::

## Turning it off

Dial the "turn off" code from the same phone. Forwarding stays on until you turn it off, including across restarts.
