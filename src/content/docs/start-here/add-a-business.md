Each business in ViFi has its own agent, phone number, calls, settings, and team. If you run more than one, add one for each and switch between them. The website calls each one a **workspace**; the ViFi app calls it a **business**.

:::tip[Joining your employer's business?]
Don't add one. Adding a business starts a separate set-up with its own agent and calls. Ask them to invite you instead: see [Join a workspace you were invited to](/start-here/join-a-workspace/).
:::

## Who can add a business

Anyone with a ViFi account and a confirmed email address. You become the new business's Admin and owner, and your other businesses stay as they are.

If you don't belong to any business yet, you can create one or wait for an invitation. The screen you see after signing in offers both. See [When you sign in without a workspace](/start-here/join-a-workspace/#when-you-sign-in-without-a-workspace).

## On the website

1. Open the workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar.
2. Choose **Create Workspace**.
3. Enter the **Business Name** and click **Create Workspace**.

The new workspace opens and the Setup Guide starts. See [The Setup Guide, step by step](/start-here/setup-guide/).

## In the ViFi app

- **Your first business.** After you confirm your email, the screen says **Set up your business**. Enter the **Business name** and tap **Create your business**. If a business has invited you, its invitation comes first, with **Create a new business** underneath.
- **Another business.** Open **More**, then **Add another business**. If you already have several, it's **Switch business**, then **Create another business**. You can also tap the business name at the top of **Home** and choose **Add another business**.

On the **New business** screen, enter the **Business name** and tap **Create business**. The app opens the new business and starts the Setup guide.

## Which business opens when you sign in

ViFi opens the business you used last. Switching businesses, creating one, or joining one changes which business that is, on the website and in the app alike. Declining an invitation doesn't.

To switch on the website, use the workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar.

In the [ViFi app](/start-here/mobile-app/) <span class="status-pill status-pill--coming-soon">Coming soon</span>, tap the business name at the top of **Home**. The **Your businesses** list shows your role in each one and any invitations waiting for you. Tap a business to open it, and the app says "Switched to Lopez Plumbing". If you tap a notification about another business, the app switches to it first. If the app ever asks you to choose a business, the one you used last is marked **Last used**.

## The limit on businesses that aren't live yet

You can have up to **3** businesses at a time that are still being set up or in their free trial. The count includes:

- businesses you created that are still being set up, in their trial, or whose trial has ended;
- businesses like these that you removed in the last 30 days.

Giving a business to another owner doesn't take it off your count.:purchase[ Businesses that have had a paid plan don't count.]

When you reach the limit, ViFi says, for example: "You have 3 businesses that are still being set up or in their trial. Finish setting one up before adding another." If businesses you removed are part of the count, it adds "Businesses you removed in the last 30 days still count." When only removed businesses are holding you back, it says to try again later.

- On the website, **Switch workspace** takes you back to your workspaces.
- In the app, the businesses being counted are listed under **Not live yet**. Tap one that's still being set up to carry on with its setup.

## Other messages you might see

- **"Please verify your email address before creating a business."** Click the link in your verification email, then try again. In the app: "Confirm your email first."
- **"You've added several businesses recently."** You can add up to 5 in a day. The message says how long to wait.
- **"Adding businesses is paused right now. Try again later."** ViFi has paused new businesses for a short while. Nothing is wrong with your account.
- **"Something else is updating your account. Try again in a moment."** Another change to your account was still being saved. Try again.

## Removing a business

If you added a business by mistake, email [support@vifi.us](mailto:support@vifi.us) and we'll delete it. To close a business you no longer need, see [Change or cancel your plan](/billing/change-or-cancel/).
