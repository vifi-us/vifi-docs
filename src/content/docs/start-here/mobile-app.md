The ViFi app puts your calls and your agent on your phone. It uses the same account and businesses as the website, so a change made in one shows up in the other. The app calls each business a **business**; the website calls it a **workspace**.

## Signing in

Sign in with your email and password, with **Continue with Google**, or on iPhone with **Continue with Apple**. Use the same method you use on the website, so you land in the same account.

If someone invited you to their business, open the invitation link from the email on your phone. It opens the app, where you can join or decline. See [Join a workspace you were invited to](/start-here/join-a-workspace/).

## Your businesses

The app opens the business you used last. To switch, tap the business name at the top of **Home**, or open **More**, then **Switch business**. To add one, choose **Add another business** at the bottom of the list on Home. See [Add a business](/start-here/add-a-business/).

## What's where

- **Home**: whether your agent is answering, anything that needs your attention, today's appointments and missed calls, a few numbers, and your latest calls. See [Home and Insights in the app](/calls-and-callers/app-home/).
- **Calls**: every call with its summary, transcript, and recording, plus your callers.
- **Messages**: text conversations on your ViFi number.
- **Agent**: everything about how your agent answers. See [Your agent in the app](#your-agent-in-the-app).
- **More**: appointments, blocked and approved callers, team, billing, notifications, your account, your businesses and invitations, this Help Center, and support.

A few settings, such as integrations, automations, and your agent's policies, open the ViFi website inside the app.

## Calls and messages

- **Search** calls by the caller's name, what they called about, or part of the number.
- **Names**: when your agent learned a caller's name, calls and texts show it with the number underneath.
- **Swipe right** on a call to call back from your own phone; **swipe left** to archive it.
- After a test call, **See what your agent understood** opens the call.

See [Call history](/calls-and-callers/call-history/#in-the-vifi-app), [Callers](/calls-and-callers/callers/), and [Messages](/calls-and-callers/messages/).

## Appointments

Open **More**, then **Appointments** to see upcoming and past appointments, open one to move, cancel, or mark it done, turn booking on or off, and set days off for bookings. See [Appointments in the app](/your-agent/appointments/#in-the-vifi-app).

## Notifications

Open **More**, then **Notifications**, and tap **Turn on notifications**. Your phone can then alert you when a call is missed, after each call with a summary, when your agent books, moves, or cancels an appointment, and when someone invites you to another business. Choose which ones you want in the same place. See [Notifications](/calls-and-callers/notifications/).

## Invitations

When another business invites you, the **More** tab shows a number, **Home** shows a banner, and the business switcher lists the invitation at the top. Open it to **Join** or **Decline**. See [Join a workspace you were invited to](/start-here/join-a-workspace/).

## When you're offline

Without a connection, the app shows "Offline — showing saved info" and keeps what it last loaded. Changes need a connection: nothing you change offline is saved for later, so try again once you're back online.

## Billing

Admins can see the business's billing status under **More**, then **Billing & plan**: the plan, usage this period, trial and renewal dates, and the payment method. Tap a paid invoice to view or download it. On some phones you can also update your payment details there.

:::purchase
Plans are chosen, changed, and cancelled on the ViFi website, not in the app. Sign in at [app.vifi.us](https://app.vifi.us/billing) and open **Billing**. See [Plans and what's included](/billing/plans-and-pricing/) and [Change or cancel your plan](/billing/change-or-cancel/).

Pages that open inside the app, including this Help Center, leave out prices and plan choices. Open ViFi in your regular web browser to see them.
:::

## Your account

**More**, then **Account & security** has your name, password, two-step verification, and the devices you're signed in on. **Mobile number** is where you add or verify the number ViFi texts, and turn texts back on after replying STOP. You can also delete your account there. See [Your account](/team-and-account/your-account/#your-mobile-number-in-the-app).

## Getting help

**More** has **Help Center**, which opens this site, and **Contact support**, which starts an email to [support@vifi.us](mailto:support@vifi.us).
