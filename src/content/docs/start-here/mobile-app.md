The ViFi app puts your calls and your agent on your phone. It uses the same account and workspaces as the website, so a change made in one shows up in the other.

## Signing in

Sign in with your email and password, with **Continue with Google**, or on iPhone with **Continue with Apple**. Use the same method you use on the website, so you land in the same account.

If someone invited you to their business, open the invitation link from the email on your phone. It opens the app, where you can join. See [Join a workspace you were invited to](/start-here/join-a-workspace/).

## What's where

- **Home**: setup progress, anything that needs your attention, and recent calls.
- **Calls**: every call with its summary, transcript, and recording, plus your callers.
- **Messages**: text conversations on your ViFi number.
- **Agent**: greeting, voice, personality, business details, call handling, transfers, and the knowledge base.
- **More**: appointments, blocked and approved callers, team, billing, notifications, your account, other workspaces and invitations, this Help Center, and support.

A few settings, such as integrations and automations, open the ViFi website inside the app.

## Notifications

Open **More**, then **Notifications**, and tap **Turn on notifications**. Your phone can then alert you when a call is missed, after each call with a summary, and when someone invites you to another business. Choose which ones you want in the same place. See [Notifications](/calls-and-callers/notifications/).

## Invitations

When another business invites you, the **More** tab shows a number, **Home** shows a banner, and **Switch workspace** lists the invitation at the top. Tap it to join or decline. See [Join a workspace you were invited to](/start-here/join-a-workspace/).

## Billing

Admins can see the workspace's billing status under **More**, then **Billing & plan**: the plan, usage this period, trial and renewal dates, and the payment method. Tap a paid invoice to view or download it. On some phones you can also update your payment details there.

:::purchase
Plans are chosen, changed, and cancelled on the ViFi website, not in the app. Sign in at [app.vifi.us](https://app.vifi.us/billing) and open **Billing**. See [Plans and what's included](/billing/plans-and-pricing/) and [Change or cancel your plan](/billing/change-or-cancel/).

Pages that open inside the app, including this Help Center, leave out prices and plan choices. Open ViFi in your regular web browser to see them.
:::

## Your account

**More**, then **Account & security** has your name, password, two-step verification, and the devices you're signed in on. You can also delete your account there. See [Your account](/team-and-account/your-account/).

## Getting help

**More** has **Help Center**, which opens this site, and **Contact support**, which starts an email to [support@vifi.us](mailto:support@vifi.us).
