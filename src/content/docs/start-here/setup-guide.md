The Setup Guide is the checklist ViFi opens after you create a workspace. It has four steps and finishes when your agent completes its first real call. You can leave and come back at any time from **Setup Guide** in the sidebar.

::screenshot[The Setup Guide with its four steps across the top: Business, Number, Agent, Call]

## Step 1: Find your business

1. Start typing your business name. ViFi searches Google's business listings. Pick yours from the list.
2. ViFi fills in your website from the listing. If it's missing or wrong, type it in.

The moment you pick a listing, ViFi starts reading your website and Google listing in the background to learn about your business: what you do, your services, and your hours. You review everything before the agent answers a call.

If your business isn't listed, skip the search and enter the name by hand. You'll fill in the rest in step 3.

## Step 2: Get your number

1. ViFi suggests an area code based on your business phone. Change it if you like.
2. Pick one of the available numbers shown.

ViFi sets the number up while you carry on to step 3. This usually takes under a minute. If it's taking longer than usual, the guide says so; if it fails, you'll see a message with a way to contact support.

:::note
No payment is needed. During the free trial only [approved callers](/calls-and-callers/approved-callers/) can reach this number, so you can test without strangers calling in.
:::

## Step 3: Meet your agent

Everything on this page was drafted from what ViFi learned in step 1. Read it and fix anything that's off.

- **Business name, description, and services.** What the agent tells callers about you.
- **Business hours.** Set each day to open or closed with times. Callers who ask "are you open on Saturday?" get answered from this.
- **Agent name.** How the agent introduces itself, for example "Alex".
- **Voice.** Pick from the recommended voices and press play to hear each one.
- **Greeting.** The first thing callers hear. The chips insert your business name, the agent name, and the recording notice for you.
- **What your agent can do.** A checklist of capabilities with an on-off switch for each:
  - Take messages
  - Answer hours questions
  - Answer from your knowledge base
  - Look up menu and services
  - Transfer to a human (off during the trial, see below)
  - Text callers
- **Stay in the loop.** Turn on **Text me a summary after each call** to get a short recap on your phone. Expand it to add email or a daily summary instead.

:::caution[Transfers during the trial]
Transferring a call to a real person is switched off until you subscribe. Everything else works during the trial. See [Transfers](/your-agent/transfers/).
:::

::screenshot[Step 3 with the business profile on the left and the agent name, voice and greeting on the right]

## Step 4: Call your agent

Your own mobile number is already on the approved list, so:

1. Call the number shown on the screen from your phone.
2. Watch the status change from ringing to in progress to completed.
3. When you hang up, the summary and transcript appear right there.

You can add up to two more approved numbers on this step, for a colleague or a second phone.

The guide completes on its own once a call finishes. If you'd rather skip the test call, use **Finish setup without a test call**. Nothing is blocked either way.

## After the guide

You land on the dashboard with a short "make it yours" checklist: add policies, review the knowledge base, connect your tools, and subscribe. A good order is in [Your first week](/start-here/first-week-checklist/).

## Trouble?

- The number step says it's taking longer than usual: give it a few minutes, then see [Calls aren't reaching ViFi](/fix-a-problem/calls-not-reaching-vifi/).
- Your call went to voicemail or didn't connect: check that you're calling from a number on the [approved list](/calls-and-callers/approved-callers/).
- The agent knew nothing about your business: step 1 may have been skipped. Fill in the profile in [Business profile](/your-agent/business-profile/).
