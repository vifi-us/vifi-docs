If a business that uses ViFi invited you, you join their workspace. **You don't need to create a business of your own.** Creating one starts a separate business with its own agent, calls, and team, which isn't what you want if you work there.

## The invitation email

The email comes from ViFi and says who invited you, for example "Maria Lopez invited you to join Lopez Plumbing on ViFi".

- The link in it works for **7 days**. The email says when it expires.
- If the business sends the invitation again, only the newest email's link works. Use that one.
- The link starts with `https://app.vifi.us/join`. On a phone with the [ViFi app](/start-here/mobile-app/) installed, it opens the app. Otherwise it opens in your browser.

Opening the link doesn't join you on its own. You always confirm first, so nothing happens if a mail filter opens the link to check it.

## If you don't have a ViFi account yet

1. Open the link in the invitation email.
2. Either enter your name and a password and click **Create account and join**, or continue with Google or Apple, for example **Continue with Google to join Lopez Plumbing**.
3. The business's workspace opens. There's no separate verification email: the invitation link already proved the address is yours.

Your account uses the email address the invitation was sent to. That's true even if the Google or Apple login you continue with has a different address. Afterwards, sign in the same way you joined: with that password, or with the same Google or Apple button.

## If you already have a ViFi account

If the invited address already has a ViFi account, the page says **Sign in to join Lopez Plumbing**. Sign in with your password, Google, or Apple, then confirm on the **Join Lopez Plumbing** card.

If you're already signed in with a different account, the page says which account you're signed in as and which address the invitation is for. Choose **Switch account** and sign in with the invited address. The invitation stays open while you switch.

## Which Google or Apple account to use

ViFi matches invitations to people by email address. The easiest path is to use the login that has the invited address: for a work address on Google Workspace, that's your work Google account, not a personal Gmail.

If your login's address is different:

- **Start from the invitation link.** Continuing with Google or Apple from the link creates your account with the invited address, whatever address the login has.
- **An existing account comes first.** If your Google or Apple login is already connected to a ViFi account, or its address already has a ViFi account, ViFi signs you in to that account. If that account's address isn't the invited one, use **Switch account**.
- **Without the link**, ViFi only shows invitations sent to your account's own address. While ViFi is invite-only, a Google or Apple login that has no ViFi account gets a message that ViFi is invite-only. Open the invitation link and continue from there.

### Apple's Hide My Email

If you choose **Hide My Email**, Apple gives ViFi a private relay address instead of your real one. An invitation sent to your work address won't match it. Either start from the invitation link and continue with Apple there, or sign in with the invited account. If ViFi turned the relay address away, Apple usually asks again next time whether to share or hide your email.

## When you sign in without a workspace

If you sign in and don't belong to a workspace yet, ViFi shows what fits your situation:

- **You've been invited.** Your invitations come first, each showing the business, your role, and who invited you. Choose **Join**. If you really do want a separate business as well, **Create a new business** is underneath.
- **Invited by your employer?** No invitation has reached your address yet. As the card says: "You don't need to create a business. Ask them to invite *your address* — invitations appear here automatically." Use **Check again** once they've sent it, or **Use a different account** if they invited another address.
- **Waiting for an invitation.** ViFi is invite-only right now, so you can't create a business yourself. Ask the business to invite the address shown, then use **Check again**.
- **Your invitation expired.** Invitations last 7 days. Ask the business to invite you again.
- **Couldn't check your invitations.** A connection problem. Use **Retry**.

New invitations appear on this screen within about a minute, without refreshing.

## When you already use ViFi

Invitations to another business show up wherever you are.

**On the website:**

- A number appears on the workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar and on its **Workspace invitations** item.
- A banner on the dashboard says who invited you, for example "Maria Lopez invited you to join Lopez Plumbing". Choose **Review** to see the invitation. If you close the banner, it stays away until a new invitation arrives.

**In the [ViFi app](/start-here/mobile-app/):** <span class="status-pill status-pill--coming-soon">Coming soon</span>

- A number appears on the **More** tab, and the same banner appears on **Home**. Tapping it opens **More**, then **Invitations**.
- **Switch workspace** lists your invitations at the top.
- If notifications are on, your phone shows **Workspace invitation**: "Maria Lopez invited you to join Lopez Plumbing". Tap it to open your invitations. You can turn these off under **Notifications**, **Workspace invitations**. See [Notifications](/calls-and-callers/notifications/).

Joining adds the business to your workspace switcher and opens it. Your other workspaces stay as they are. To turn an invitation down, decline it; if you change your mind, ask the business to invite you again.

## Something went wrong

- **"Invitation unavailable"**: the link expired, was cancelled, was already used, or was replaced by a newer email. Ask the business for a new invitation.
- **You created a business by mistake**: it doesn't stop you joining. Join the invited workspace from the switcher, and email [support@vifi.us](mailto:support@vifi.us) if you'd like the extra one deleted.
- **Nothing arrives**: check spam, then ask the person who invited you to check the address on their **Team** page. See [Team and roles](/team-and-account/team-and-roles/).

## For technical users

- Invitation links look like `https://app.vifi.us/join?token=…`. Emails sent before this change use `https://app.vifi.us/register?token=…`; those keep working until they expire.
- The app opens these links through universal links (iPhone) and verified app links (Android). Some mail security services rewrite links so they always open in a browser. The web page works the same way.
