The Setup Guide gets your agent answering. This list makes it good. Do one or two items a day.

## Day 1: listen and adjust

- [ ] Read the briefing from your test call. Check the summary matched what you said.
- [ ] Call again and ask the three questions your customers ask most. Note anything the agent got wrong or couldn't answer.
- [ ] Fix the greeting if it sounded stiff. Shorter is better. See [Greeting and personality](/your-agent/greeting-and-personality/).
- [ ] Set your business hours if the Setup Guide didn't get them right. See [Business profile](/your-agent/business-profile/).

## Day 2: teach it

- [ ] Open **Knowledge Base** and look at **Unanswered**. Every question there is one a caller asked that the agent couldn't answer. Convert each into an article. See [Knowledge base](/your-agent/knowledge-base/).
- [ ] Write three to five policies in plain English, such as "Never quote a price; say the office will follow up." See [Call behavior and policies](/your-agent/call-behavior/).
- [ ] Add tricky names and product words to the [pronunciation guide](/your-agent/pronunciation/).

## Day 3: route calls where they belong

- [ ] Forward your business line. See [Forward your calls](/start-here/forward-your-calls/).
- [ ] Decide who gets notified and how: email, text, Slack, or Discord. See [Notifications](/calls-and-callers/notifications/).
- [ ] Invite the people who need to see calls. See [Team and roles](/team-and-account/team-and-roles/).

## Day 4: connect a tool

- [ ] Connect your calendar so the agent can book, or your CRM so it recognises callers. See [Connect your tools](/connect-your-tools/overview/).

## Before the trial ends

- [ ] Decide whether calls should be recorded and what the recording notice says. See [Recording and compliance](/your-agent/recording-and-compliance/).
- [ ] Subscribe so your number keeps answering after the trial and transfers to a person switch on. See [Your free trial](/billing/free-trial/).
- [ ] Set a spending alert so extra usage never surprises you. See [Usage and guardrails](/billing/usage-and-guardrails/).
- [ ] Set your transfer number so callers who insist on a person get one. See [Transfers](/your-agent/transfers/).

## Every week after

- [ ] Skim **Unanswered** in the knowledge base and convert what's useful.
- [ ] Read a handful of calls with negative sentiment. They're where the agent needs help. See [Review your first calls](/best-practices/review-your-first-calls/).
