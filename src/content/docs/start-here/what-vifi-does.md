ViFi is an AI phone assistant for small businesses. It answers your calls, helps the caller the way a good front-desk person would, and sends you a written recap of every conversation.

You keep your existing phone number. Calls you can't take get forwarded to ViFi, or you can hand out your ViFi number directly.

## What happens on a call

1. **Someone calls.** The call reaches your ViFi number, either directly or because your phone forwarded it.
2. **Your agent answers** with the greeting you wrote, in the voice you chose, using the name you gave it.
3. **It helps.** Depending on what you've turned on, the agent can answer questions about your business and hours, look up answers in your knowledge base or menu, take a message, book an appointment through a connected calendar, or transfer the caller to a real person.
4. **It wraps up** when the caller is done, or when it can't help and has taken a message.
5. **You get the briefing.** Within a minute or two you have a summary, action items, the full transcript, and a recording if recording is on. ViFi can email or text you, post to Slack or Discord, and log the call in your CRM.

## What your agent knows

Your agent only knows what you tell it. It draws on:

- **Your business profile.** Name, description, services, hours, and industry. ViFi fills most of this in from your website and Google listing during setup, and you can edit all of it.
- **Your knowledge base.** Short question-and-answer articles you write, documents you upload, and web pages you point it at.
- **Your menu or services list**, if you keep one in ViFi.
- **Your policies.** Plain-English rules such as "never quote a price over the phone" or "always offer to transfer refund requests".
- **Connected tools.** A calendar for booking, a CRM for recognising callers, and so on.

The more of this you fill in, the fewer calls end in "let me take a message".

## What it will not do

- It never invents policies, prices, or availability it hasn't been given. When it doesn't know, it takes a message or offers a transfer.
- It follows your policies over anything a caller asks for.
- It won't send texts on your behalf beyond ViFi's fixed follow-up receipt until you enable more. See [Messages](/calls-and-callers/messages/).

## What you need to get started

- A business phone number you can forward, or a willingness to hand out a new number.
- About ten minutes for the [Setup Guide](/start-here/setup-guide/).
- Your own mobile phone, so you can call your agent and hear it answer.

No card is needed for the free trial. See [Your free trial](/billing/free-trial/) for what's included.
