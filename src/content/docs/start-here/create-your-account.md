## What you'll need

- An email address you can check right away.
- A password of at least ten characters, or a Google account.

## Sign up

1. Go to [app.vifi.us/register](https://app.vifi.us/register).
2. Enter your email and choose a password, or click **Sign in with Google**.
3. Complete the "I'm not a robot" check if one appears.
4. Check your inbox for a verification email and click the link. If it doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check spam, then use **Resend** on the verification screen.

## Create your workspace

A workspace is where you set up and manage the AI agent for one business. After you verify your email, ViFi asks for your **business name** and creates the workspace. You become its **Admin**.

If you run more than one business, you can create a workspace for each and switch between them from the top of the sidebar.

## Joining someone else's workspace

If a colleague invited you, use the link in the invitation email. The email address on the form is locked to the address that was invited, so sign up with that one. Once you accept, their workspace appears in your workspace switcher.

If you created an account without an invitation and expected to see a workspace, ask the workspace Admin to invite the exact email you signed up with. See [Team and roles](/team-and-account/team-and-roles/).

## "Registration is invite-only"

ViFi can be switched to invite-only mode. If you see this message, you need an invitation link to create an account. Email [support@vifi.us](mailto:support@vifi.us) to request access.

## What happens next

The Setup Guide opens automatically. It takes about ten minutes and ends with you calling your own agent. Continue with [The Setup Guide, step by step](/start-here/setup-guide/).
