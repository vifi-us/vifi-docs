Open **Team** in the sidebar. Admins can invite people, change roles, and remove members.

## Inviting someone

1. Click **Invite team member**.
2. Enter their email address and choose a role.
3. Send. They get an email with a link. If they don't have a ViFi account, the link lets them create one with that email address.

::screenshot[The Team page with the member list and the invite form]

:::note[Known issue]
Invitations that haven't been accepted yet don't appear in the list, so you can't see or cancel a pending invite from this page. If someone didn't get their invitation, check their spam folder, then send it again.
:::

## Roles

| | Admin | Member | Viewer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Workspace settings: profile, hours, voice, agent configuration | Full | Read only | Read only |
| Billing: subscription, payment method, invoices | Full | Not allowed | Not allowed |
| Calls: history, transcripts, recordings | Full | Read only | Read only |
| Knowledge base and menu | Full | Full | Read only |
| Tools and integrations | Full | Read only | Read only |
| Team members: invite, change roles, remove | Full | Read only | Read only |
| Analytics | Yes | Yes | No |
| Audit log | Yes | Yes | No |

The **Role permissions** panel on the Team page shows the same table with the exact current rules.

Which role to give:

- **Admin** for owners and managers who handle billing and configuration.
- **Member** for staff who read calls and keep the knowledge base current.
- **Viewer** for anyone who only needs to read calls, such as an outside bookkeeper.

## Changing a role or removing someone

Use the menu at the end of a member's row. The workspace owner can't be removed. Removing someone signs them out of the workspace immediately; their account still exists for any other workspace they belong to.

## More than one business

Each business gets its own workspace with its own team. A person can belong to several workspaces and switch between them from the top of the sidebar.
