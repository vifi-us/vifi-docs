## Passwords

Passwords must be at least ten characters. Change yours under **Account**, then **Security**. If you've forgotten it, use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page; a reset link goes to your email.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor adds a code to your sign-in so a stolen password isn't enough. Set it up under **Account**, then **Security**. Options:

- **Authenticator app.** Scan the QR code with Google Authenticator, 1Password, Authy, or similar. Recommended.
- **Text message.** A code to your verified mobile number. Requires a phone number on your account.
- **Email.** A code to your email address.

You can enable more than one and choose a preferred method.

## Backup codes

When you enable two-factor, ViFi gives you backup codes. Save them somewhere safe. Each works once, and they're the way back in if you lose your phone. You can regenerate them any time, which invalidates the old set.

## Sign-in alerts

ViFi emails and texts you when your account is signed in from an unfamiliar device or when two-factor settings change. These alerts can't be turned off.

## Passkeys

Sign in with a fingerprint, face, or security key instead of a password. <span class="status-pill status-pill--coming-soon">Coming soon</span>

## Locked out?

- Too many wrong codes locks two-factor for a short while. Wait and try again.
- Lost your phone and your backup codes: email [support@vifi.us](mailto:support@vifi.us) from the email on the account. Support will verify it's you before removing two-factor.
- See also [I can't sign in](/fix-a-problem/cant-sign-in/).
