Open **Channels** in the sidebar. It shows every way customers can reach your agent.

## Your phone number

The number ViFi assigned during the Setup Guide is shown at the top. Give it out directly, or forward your existing line to it. See [Forward your calls](/start-here/forward-your-calls/).

If it says **No phone number assigned**, the Setup Guide's number step was skipped or is still in progress. Return to **Setup Guide** to pick one. If provisioning failed, the page offers to notify you when the number goes live, and you can email support.

::screenshot[The Channels page with the assigned number and the Additional numbers section]

## Additional numbers

You can request extra numbers, for example a separate number for a second location or an advertising campaign. All numbers ring the same agent and appear in the same call history. Extra numbers may carry a monthly charge; see [Plans and pricing](/billing/plans-and-pricing/).

## Keeping your existing number

ViFi doesn't move your existing number. Your carrier keeps it and forwards calls to ViFi under your control. If you cancel ViFi, turn off forwarding and everything is as it was.

## Website widget

The **Embed widget** section on this page is where you create keys and get the snippet for your website. See [Website widget](/connect-your-tools/website-widget/).
