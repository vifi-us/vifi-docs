Open your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Account**. Everything here is about you as a person. Workspace settings, like the business profile, live under **Settings**.

## Profile

Your name and email. Changing the email requires verifying the new address.

## Connected logins

Shows whether Google sign-in is linked to your account. Link it to sign in with one click; unlink it if you'd rather use a password only. You must keep at least one way to sign in.

## Phone number

Add and verify a mobile number to receive sign-in codes and account alerts by text. ViFi sends a code to confirm the number. Only verified numbers are used, and you can remove the number any time.

By tapping **Send code** you agree to receive texts from ViFi for sign-in codes, account alerts, and security notifications. Reply STOP to any of them to opt out.

## Your mobile number in the app

<span class="status-pill status-pill--coming-soon">Coming soon</span> In the [ViFi app](/start-here/mobile-app/), open **More**, then **Account & security**. The **Mobile number** row shows your number and whether texts are on, or **Add your mobile number** if you haven't added one.

### Adding and verifying a number

1. Tap **Add your mobile number**.
2. Type the number. US numbers don't need a country code; elsewhere, start with `+`.
3. Read the consent line under the field, then tap **Send code**: "By tapping Send code, you consent to receive SMS messages from ViFi at this number for sign-in codes, account alerts, and security notifications. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help."
4. Type the 6-digit code from the text and tap **Verify**. The code expires in 10 minutes. If it doesn't arrive, **Send a new code** works again after a minute.

Once it's verified, **Choose your text alerts** takes you to **Notifications**, where you pick which alerts come by text.

### Changing or removing the number

- **Change number** sends a code to the new number. Until you verify it, your current number keeps getting your sign-in codes and alerts.
- **Remove number** stops all texts from ViFi until you add and verify a number again. If your two-step sign-in codes come by text, switch two-step verification to another method first; the app offers **Open two-step settings**.

### Turning texts back on after STOP

If you replied STOP to a ViFi text, the row says "Texts are off (you replied STOP)" and the screen shows **Texts from ViFi are off**.

1. Open **Mobile number**.
2. Tap **Turn texts back on**.

Sign-in codes, billing alerts, and security alerts go to that number again, and your other alert choices come back as they were. While texts are off, **Change number** is hidden: turn texts back on first, or remove the number and add the new one. If texts still don't arrive, reply **START** to any ViFi text.

## Security

Change your password and set up two-factor authentication. See [Security and sign-in](/team-and-account/security/).

## Active sessions

Every device where you're signed in, with the last time it was used. Sign out a device you don't recognise.

## Recent activity

A log of security events on your account: sign-ins, password changes, verification, two-factor changes. If you see something you didn't do, change your password and contact [support@vifi.us](mailto:support@vifi.us).

## Deleting your account

Deleting removes your access to every workspace. It doesn't delete the workspaces themselves; another Admin keeps them. If you're the only Admin, transfer that role first. To delete a whole workspace and its data, see [Change or cancel your plan](/billing/change-or-cancel/).
