Open your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Account**. Everything here is about you as a person. Workspace settings, like the business profile, live under **Settings**.

## Profile

Your name and email. Changing the email requires verifying the new address.

## Connected logins

Shows whether Google sign-in is linked to your account. Link it to sign in with one click; unlink it if you'd rather use a password only. You must keep at least one way to sign in.

## Phone number

Add and verify a mobile number to receive sign-in codes and account alerts by text. ViFi sends a code to confirm the number. Only verified numbers are used, and you can remove the number any time.

By tapping **Send code** you agree to receive texts from ViFi for sign-in codes, account alerts, and security notifications. Reply STOP to any of them to opt out.

## Security

Change your password and set up two-factor authentication. See [Security and sign-in](/team-and-account/security/).

## Active sessions

Every device where you're signed in, with the last time it was used. Sign out a device you don't recognise.

## Recent activity

A log of security events on your account: sign-ins, password changes, verification, two-factor changes. If you see something you didn't do, change your password and contact [support@vifi.us](mailto:support@vifi.us).

## Deleting your account

**Delete account** is at the bottom of the **Profile** tab. Deleting your account signs you out everywhere and gives you 30 days to change your mind; signing in before then cancels it. After that, your name, email, and phone number are erased.

- **Businesses only you use** are paused right away and closed for good with your account.
- **Businesses where you're a member** keep running, and you're removed from them.
- **A business you own that other people use** blocks the deletion. Make one of them the owner first, from the menu on their row on the **Team** page.

For the steps in the app, on the website, or by email, and what's kept afterwards, see [Delete your account](/team-and-account/delete-your-account/).
