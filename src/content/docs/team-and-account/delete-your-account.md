This page is for anyone with an account in **ViFi**, the AI phone receptionist made by **ViFi LLC**. That covers the website at app.vifi.us and the ViFi app for iPhone and Android. It explains how to delete your account and what happens to your data afterwards.

There are three ways to delete your account, and you don't need the app for any of them:

- [In the ViFi app](#in-the-vifi-app)
- [On the website](#on-the-website)
- [By email](#by-email), if you can't sign in or would rather not

Deletion isn't immediate. You get 30 days to change your mind, and then the account is deleted for good.

## Before you start

- **Businesses only you use are closed with your account.** A business (also called a workspace) that has no other members stops answering calls, and its ViFi number is released. To keep a business running, invite a teammate and make them the owner first.
- **You can't delete your account while you own a business other people use.** Make one of them the owner first. On the website, open **Team**, use the menu at the end of their row, and click **Make Owner**. In the app, open **More**, then **Team**, tap the person, then tap **Make owner**.
- **Turn off call forwarding** on your business line so calls ring your own phone again. See [Forward your calls](/start-here/forward-your-calls/).
- **Signed up with Google or Apple and never set a password?** You'll need to have signed in within the last 15 minutes. If ViFi asks you to sign in again, sign out, sign back in, and start over.

## In the ViFi app

<span class="status-pill status-pill--coming-soon">Coming soon</span> The app is being tested and isn't in the App Store or Google Play yet.

1. Open **More**, then **Account & security**.
2. Under **Danger zone**, tap **Delete my account**.
3. Check which businesses will be closed and which ones you'll leave.
4. If your account has a password, enter it. Then type `DELETE` in the box.
5. Tap **Delete my account**, then tap **Delete account** to confirm.

The app shows the date your account will be deleted. Tap **Done** to sign out.

## On the website

1. Sign in at [app.vifi.us/account](https://app.vifi.us/account#delete-account). You can also get there by clicking your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Account**.
2. On the **Profile** tab, scroll down to **Delete account** and click **Delete account**.
3. Read what will happen. If your account has a password, enter it. Then type `DELETE`.
4. Click **Delete account**.

You're signed out, and the sign-in page shows the date your account will be deleted.

## By email

Email [support@vifi.us](mailto:support@vifi.us) from the address you sign in with, and put "Delete my ViFi account" in the subject. If you can't use that address any more, say so and include your business name, and we'll ask for something else to confirm the account is yours.

A person reads every message, and most replies go out within one business day. Once we've confirmed the request, your account gets the same 30 days described below.

## The 30 days after you ask

As soon as you ask:

- You're signed out on every device, and your phones stop getting ViFi notifications.
- Each business that only you use is paused. Your agent stops answering its calls and nobody can sign in to it. Billing stops too, so nothing is charged. Its ViFi number is held for you.
- ViFi emails you the deletion date, with a **Sign in to cancel** button.

**Changed your mind?** Sign in before that date the way you normally do. That cancels the deletion, brings your paused businesses back as they were, and ViFi emails you to confirm.

## What's deleted

When the 30 days are up, ViFi deletes the account within a day and emails you once it's done.

| Data | What happens |
|---|---|
| Your name, email address, password, and mobile number | Erased. All that's left of your account is an ID with no name or contact details. |
| Google and Apple sign-in links, two-step verification, and your notification and text-alert settings | Deleted. ViFi also tells Apple to revoke Sign in with Apple for ViFi. |
| The phones you got notifications on, with their notification tokens and device names | Deleted on the day you ask. |
| Your membership in businesses you don't own | Removed. Things you added there, such as articles or notes, stay with that business. Its audit log shows "Deleted User" instead of your name. |
| Businesses that only you used | Closed for good. The ViFi number is released, any subscription is cancelled, and nobody can open the business again. |
| Call recordings and files uploaded to the knowledge base in those businesses | Deleted. |
| Call history, transcripts, summaries, caller profiles, text messages, knowledge base articles, and settings in those businesses | Marked as deleted and hidden from everyone. ViFi doesn't yet erase these records automatically. To have them erased, email [support@vifi.us](mailto:support@vifi.us). |

## What's kept, and for how long

- **Billing records**, such as invoices and payments, are kept for as long as tax and accounting rules require. Our payment processor keeps its own copies under the same rules.
- **Security and delivery records** stay with the account's ID: the log of sign-ins and security events (times, IP addresses, and browser or device), and the record of emails and texts ViFi sent you. We keep them to protect the service, look into misuse, and show what was sent with your consent. They aren't deleted on a schedule.
- **Usage analytics** are tied to the account's ID. If you used the ViFi website, our analytics service also has the name and email address from those visits. Email [support@vifi.us](mailto:support@vifi.us) to have them removed.
- **Backups** of ViFi's database are deleted after 30 days, so anything erased is gone from backups within 30 days.

## Deleting some data and keeping your account

- **A call recording:** open the call and delete the recording, on the website or in the app. To change how long recordings are kept, see [Recording and compliance](/your-agent/recording-and-compliance/).
- **A phone that gets your notifications:** sign out of the app on that phone.
- **A caller's details, or anything else:** email [support@vifi.us](mailto:support@vifi.us). See [Data and privacy](/reference/data-and-privacy/).

The [Privacy Policy](https://vifi.us/legal/privacy/) is the binding description of how ViFi handles personal information.
