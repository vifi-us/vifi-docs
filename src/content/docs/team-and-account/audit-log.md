The audit log records actions taken in your workspace: settings changed, knowledge base edits, team changes, recordings played, and more. Open **Settings** and click the **Audit log** card, or go to it from the link in a notification email.

::screenshot[The audit log with the actions filter open]

## Reading it

Each row shows the time, the person, the action, and what it applied to. Use the **All actions** filter to narrow to one kind of change, such as knowledge base edits.

## Who can see it

Admins and Members can read the audit log. Viewers can't.

## What it's for

- Finding out why the agent started behaving differently. Look for policy or greeting changes around that date.
- Checking that recordings are only played by the people who should.
- Keeping a record for your own compliance needs.

Entries can't be edited or deleted.
