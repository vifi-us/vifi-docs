Open any call from **Calls** or from the dashboard. The page is arranged so the most useful part is at the top.

::screenshot[A call detail page with the summary card at the top and the transcript below]

## Summary and intent

- **Conversation summary.** Two to four sentences: who called, what they wanted, and how it ended.
- **Action items.** Things someone on your side needs to do, such as "Call back Jamie about a quote for a fence repair." These also appear on the caller's profile as open follow-ups.
- **Tags.** Short labels ViFi attaches, such as the topic or outcome.
- **Caller facts.** Details the caller mentioned that are worth remembering: their address, a preferred time, the name of their dog. These are saved to the [caller's profile](/calls-and-callers/callers/) so the agent can use them next time.
- **Unanswered questions.** Anything the caller asked that the agent couldn't answer. Each one is a candidate for a [knowledge base article](/your-agent/knowledge-base/).

## Transcript

The full conversation, with the caller and your agent on alternate sides. Use it when the summary isn't enough, or to check exactly what the agent said.

## Recording

If [recording](/your-agent/recording-and-compliance/) is on, the player sits above the transcript. Recordings play from a private link that expires after a few minutes, and every play is logged. Recordings are deleted after your retention period.

## Call timeline and event timeline

- **Call timeline** shows the key moments and the outcome: answered, transferred, missed, and how long each stage took.
- **Event timeline** lists system milestones during the call. You'll rarely need it, but support may ask about it if something went wrong.

## Sharing a call

Team members can open the same page. There's no public link. To share a call with someone outside your workspace, invite them (see [Team and roles](/team-and-account/team-and-roles/)) or copy the summary into an email.
