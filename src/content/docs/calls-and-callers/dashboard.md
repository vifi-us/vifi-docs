The dashboard is the first thing you see when you sign in. It answers three questions: is my agent live, what did callers need, and what needs follow-up.

::screenshot[The dashboard with the alert strip, the three stat cards, and today at a glance]

## Alerts

The strip at the top shows anything that needs attention, and says **Nothing urgent right now** when there's nothing. You may see:

- **Billing needs attention.** A payment failed or the subscription lapsed. See [Invoices and payment](/billing/invoices-and-payment/).
- **Trial ending soon**, with the date. See [Your free trial](/billing/free-trial/).
- **Phone provisioning in progress** or **No live phone number yet.** See [Phone numbers](/team-and-account/phone-numbers/).
- **No call activity yet.** Nobody has called. Try a [test call](/your-agent/test-your-agent/).
- **Failed calls are elevated.** Several recent calls ended unsuccessfully. See [Audio problems](/fix-a-problem/audio-problems/).
- **Business hours are set to Closed every day.** Fix them in [Business profile](/your-agent/business-profile/).

## The numbers

- **Total calls** in the period.
- **Average duration** of completed calls.
- **Positive sentiment**: the share of recent calls where the caller's tone was positive, judged from the summary.

## Today at a glance

A bar per hour for the last 24 hours, so you can see when calls come in. Use it to decide when to forward calls and when you'd rather answer yourself.

## Review recent calls

The list at the bottom links to each call's detail page. **Open Call History** takes you to the full list. See [Call history](/calls-and-callers/call-history/).
