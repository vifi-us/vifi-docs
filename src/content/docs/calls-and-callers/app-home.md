**Home** is the first tab in the [ViFi app](/start-here/mobile-app/). From top to bottom it shows which business you're in, whether your agent is answering, anything that needs you, today's appointments and missed calls, a few numbers, and your latest calls. On the website, the [dashboard](/calls-and-callers/dashboard/) does the same job.

## Switching business

The business name sits under the greeting at the top. If you belong to more than one business, or have an invitation waiting, tap it to open **Your businesses**:

- Tap a business to switch to it. The app says "Switched to Lopez Plumbing".
- Invitations waiting for you are at the top: open them to join or decline.
- **Add another business** is at the bottom. See [Add a business](/start-here/add-a-business/).

Calls, settings, and team are separate for each business. If you tap a notification about another of your businesses, the app switches to it first and Home tells you it did.

## The status card

The card at the top answers one question: is my agent answering calls? It shows your agent's phone number (tap it to copy) and when the last call was.

| The card says | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Your agent is answering calls** | Callers to this number reach your agent. |
| **Answering approved callers only** | You're in the free trial, so only your approved numbers get through, for example "(2 of 3)". See [Approved callers](/calls-and-callers/approved-callers/). |
| **Your agent is on a call now** | A call is in progress. Home refreshes on its own until it ends. |
| **Your number is being set up** | Usually a minute or two. Your agent goes live as soon as it's ready. |
| **Not live yet** | Your agent needs a phone number before it can answer calls. |
| **Your agent isn't answering calls** | Calls stopped, for example because this billing period's spending limit was reached. The card says why. |
| **Payment problem** | Your last payment didn't go through, so your agent may stop answering. |
| **Your free trial has ended**, **Trial minutes used up**, or **Your plan was cancelled** | Your agent isn't answering calls. |
| **This business is closing** | The owner asked to delete their account. |

When calls have stopped for a billing reason, people who can see billing get **Open billing**, which opens **Billing & plan**. If calls are paused for another reason, **Contact support** starts an email to [support@vifi.us](mailto:support@vifi.us).

Under the card, a smaller notice shows your trial: for example "Free trial: 4 days left" with the free minutes left, and an **Approved callers** button.

## Needs attention

If something needs you, **Needs attention** lists it, most important first. The first item has a button; up to two more follow, then **Show more**. Something the status card already covers isn't repeated here.

| Item | Button |
|---|---|
| Callers were turned away during your trial because their number isn't approved | **Approved callers** |
| **Next step: get your phone number** or **Finish setting up** | **Continue setup** |
| A connected tool, such as your calendar, **needs reconnecting** | **Reconnect**, which opens the Integrations page of the website |
| **Business hours are set to Closed every day** | **Fix hours** |
| You've used most of this period's included minutes or texts | **See usage** |
| Questions your agent couldn't answer | **Teach your agent** |

People who can't change a setting see the item without the button, and some items tell them to ask a business admin.

## Today

The **Today** card appears when you have missed calls today or appointment booking is on.

- **Missed calls today.** Tap to see who to call back. It opens **Calls** with the **Missed** filter.
- **Today's appointments.** Up to three, earliest first, with the time, the customer, and the service. Tap one to open it. With more than three, **See all** opens the full list, for example **See all 5 appointments**. If nothing is booked it says **No appointments today**.

"Today" means today in your business's time zone.

## At a glance

Four numbers for your business, counted in its time zone and leaving out archived calls:

- **Calls today** and **Last 7 days**: tap either to open **Calls**.
- **Missed today**: tap to open **Calls** showing only missed calls.
- **Average call**: how long completed calls last.

Admins and Members also get **See insights** under the numbers. See [Insights](#insights) below.

## Recent calls

Your five latest calls, each with the caller's name when your agent learned it (with the number underneath) and what they wanted. Calls from the test button show as **Test call**, and calls through the button on your website as **Website visitor**. **See all** opens **Calls**.

While you're still setting up, Admins also get **Test your agent** on Home. See [Test your agent](/your-agent/test-your-agent/).

## Insights

**See insights** opens charts of your calls over time. Admins and Members can open it; Viewers can't.

Pick a range at the top: **24 hours**, **7 days**, **30 days**, or **90 days**. If there were no calls in the last 7 days, Insights starts on a longer range and says so.

- **Calls**, **Answered**, **Missed**, and **Average call** for the range.
- **Calls by day**, as answered and missed bars. For 90 days it's **Calls by week**.
- **Busiest times**: calls by hour of the day, with a sentence such as "Busiest on Tuesdays, around 10 am."
- **What callers wanted**: the most common topics, with how many calls each.
- **How callers felt**: the share of calls that were positive, neutral, mixed, or negative.
- **What your agent did**: how many answered calls ended with **Booked an appointment**, and how many with **Passed the call to your team**.

Days and hours are in your business's time zone, and the bottom of the screen names it. The [Analytics](/calls-and-callers/analytics/) page on the website has the same numbers with a few more measures.
