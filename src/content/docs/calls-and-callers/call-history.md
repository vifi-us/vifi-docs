Open **Calls** in the sidebar. Every call your agent handled is listed, newest first, including test calls from the browser.

## Finding a call

- **Search by caller number.** Type part of the number.
- **Filter** by status or by whether the call has tags.
- Click a row to open it. On wide screens the detail opens beside the list so you can move through calls quickly.

When the agent learned the caller's name on a call, the row shows the name with the number underneath.

::screenshot[The Calls list with the search box, filters, and status badges]

## Status badges

Each call shows where its analysis is:

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Processing** | The call has ended and ViFi is writing the summary. Usually under two minutes. |
| Ready (no badge) | Summary, action items, and transcript are available. |
| **No transcript** | The call ended before anyone spoke, or audio never arrived. Common with hang-ups. |
| **Unavailable** | Analysis couldn't run. If you see this often, see [I didn't get a briefing](/fix-a-problem/no-briefing/). |

## Archived calls

A call can be archived from its detail page to keep the list tidy. Archived calls show a notice at the top and can be found with the filter.

## In the ViFi app

<span class="status-pill status-pill--coming-soon">Coming soon</span> Open the **Calls** tab.

### Searching

Type in **Search name, number or topic**. It finds calls by:

- the caller's name, when the agent learned it on a call;
- what they called about, from the summary;
- part of the number, once you've typed at least three digits.

The search works within the filter you've picked: **All**, **Missed**, or **Archived**.

### Names on calls

Each call shows the caller's name when the agent learned it, with the number underneath. Calls you made from the test button show as **Test call**, and calls through the button on your website show as **Website visitor**. Otherwise you see the number, or **Unknown caller**.

### Swiping and long-pressing

- **Swipe right** on a call to **Call back**. Your phone's dialer opens, so the call comes from your own phone, not your ViFi number.
- **Swipe left** to **Archive** a call, or **Restore** an archived one. Only Admins can archive.
- **Long-press** a call for the same choices: **Call back (from your phone)** and **Archive call** or **Restore to Calls**.

Screen readers offer the same actions on each call.

On a call's detail page, **Call back (from your phone)** and **Text (from your phone)** also use your own phone. **Callers**, at the top of the list, opens everyone who called, with your blocked and approved callers.

## Who can see calls

Everyone on the team can read calls. Only roles with recording access can play recordings. See [Team and roles](/team-and-account/team-and-roles/).
