While you're on the free trial, your ViFi number only answers calls from up to three phone numbers you approve. This lets you test without strangers using your trial minutes.

Your own mobile is approved automatically during the Setup Guide.

## Managing the list

1. Open **Callers** in the sidebar, then **Approved test callers**.
2. Add a number in full, with the area code.
3. Remove a number to free up a slot.

::screenshot[The approved test callers page with three slots]

## What unapproved callers hear

Calls from any other number are not answered by the agent. If you've forwarded your business line during the trial, real customers won't get through to ViFi. Forward your line after subscribing, or test with the approved phones only. See [Forward your calls](/start-here/forward-your-calls/).

## After the trial

Once you subscribe, the page says **Your workspace accepts every caller** and the list no longer applies. To keep specific numbers out, use [Blocked callers](/calls-and-callers/blocked-callers/) instead.
