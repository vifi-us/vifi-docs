title: The agent said something wrong
description: It gave a wrong answer, made something up, gave up too early, or hung up on someone. What to change.

  order: 2
---

Open the call and read the transcript first. Then find the pattern below.

## It gave a wrong fact

The agent only knows what's in your profile, knowledge base, and menu. Find the wrong fact and fix it at the source:

- Hours, services, description: [Business profile](/your-agent/business-profile/).
- Prices and items: [Menu and services](/your-agent/menu-and-services/).
- Anything else: search the [Knowledge base](/your-agent/knowledge-base/). If two articles disagree, the agent may pick either; delete the stale one.

If the fact came from a document or web page you added, the document may be out of date. Remove it and add an article with the correct answer.

## It made something up

Add a policy such as "If you don't know an answer, say so and offer to take a message. Never guess." See [Call behavior](/your-agent/call-behavior/). Then add an article for the question it guessed at.

## It said something it shouldn't have

Add a policy that forbids it in plain words: "Never discuss pricing", "Never comment on other companies", "Don't give medical advice". Policies take effect on the next call.

## It gave up and took a message when it could have helped

- Check that the relevant capability is on under **Tools and capabilities**: hours, knowledge base, menu.
- Check the knowledge base has the answer. Look at **Unanswered** for what it searched for.
- Soften the fallback line so it offers to try again before taking a message. See [Greeting and personality](/your-agent/greeting-and-personality/).

## It said goodbye while the caller still needed help

This is a known weakness we're working on. In the meantime, a policy helps: "Only end the call after the caller confirms they have nothing else. If a transfer fails, offer to take a message, then ask if there's anything else." The **silence timeout** in [Call behavior](/your-agent/call-behavior/) may also be too short for callers who pause to look things up.

## It mispronounced a name

Add it to the [pronunciation guide](/your-agent/pronunciation/).

## It answered in the wrong language

Check the language setting and the **Switch to the caller's language** switch in [Voice and language](/your-agent/voice-and-language/).

## Still wrong?

Send the call link to [support@vifi.us](mailto:support@vifi.us) with what you expected. Transcripts make this quick to diagnose.
