Email **[support@vifi.us](mailto:support@vifi.us)**. A person reads every message. Most replies go out within one business day.

## What to include

- **Your workspace name** or the email you sign in with.
- **A link to the call**, if it's about a call. Open the call and copy the address bar.
- **What you expected and what happened**, in a sentence or two.
- **When it happened**, with your time zone, if there's no call to link.
- **A screenshot** of any error message.

## Things support can do that you can't

- Remove two-factor authentication after verifying it's you.
- Change your plan to a smaller one.
- Delete a workspace, a caller's data, or an account and its data.
- Ban an abusive caller across the whole platform.
- Look into a call that failed before it reached your workspace.
