## A text to a caller didn't send

Texts from your ViFi number to callers are currently limited to ViFi's fixed follow-up text, sent only after the caller hears a short notice and clearly agrees. If the caller didn't agree, or asked for something other than that receipt, nothing is sent. Free-form texts, links, and missed-call texts aren't available yet. See [Messages](/calls-and-callers/messages/).

If the caller did agree and nothing arrived:

- Check **Messages** for the conversation. A message shown with a warning icon was rejected by the carrier, often because the number is a landline.
- Check your text segment quota under **Billing**. See [Usage and guardrails](/billing/usage-and-guardrails/).

## A text to me didn't arrive

Summaries, sign-in codes, and alerts come from ViFi's own number.

- Your mobile must be verified under **Account**. See [Your account](/team-and-account/your-account/).
- If you ever replied STOP to a ViFi text, all texts to that number stop. Reply START, or use the re-enable button under **Settings**, then **Notifications**.
- Check the alert's switch under **Notification preferences**.
- Sign-in codes are rate limited; wait a minute before requesting another.

## A team member isn't getting SMS transcripts

Check their number is on the SMS recipients list under **Settings**, then **Notifications**, and that it's a mobile number.
