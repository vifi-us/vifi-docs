## "Invalid email or password"

Use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. A reset link goes to your email. If you signed up with Google, use **Sign in with Google** instead; there may be no password on the account.

## "Email verification required"

Your address wasn't verified after sign-up. Sign in to reach the verification screen and click **Resend**. Check spam.

## "Account temporarily locked"

Too many failed attempts. Wait fifteen minutes and try again, or reset your password.

## Two-factor code doesn't work

- Authenticator app codes depend on your phone's clock. Make sure it's set automatically.
- Text codes need mobile signal, and stop if you've replied STOP to ViFi. Choose a different method on the code screen.
- Use a backup code if you saved them. See [Security and sign-in](/team-and-account/security/).
- Lost everything: email [support@vifi.us](mailto:support@vifi.us) from the account's email address.

## Signed in but no workspace

You see a welcome screen offering to join or create a workspace. Either the invitation went to a different email than you signed in with, or the Admin hasn't sent one. Ask them to invite the exact email you used. Creating a new workspace starts a separate business set-up, which is probably not what you want if you were invited.

## "Your session has ended"

Someone signed you out of that device from **Account**, or your access to the workspace was removed. Sign in again; if you can't reach the workspace, ask its Admin.
