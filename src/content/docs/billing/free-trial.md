The trial starts when you pick a phone number in the Setup Guide. No card is needed.

## What's included

- 7 days.
- 25 AI minutes and 50 text segments.
- Everything in the product: knowledge base, integrations, recordings, the website widget, the team.

## What's held back during the trial

- **Only approved callers get through.** Up to three phone numbers you choose. See [Approved callers](/calls-and-callers/approved-callers/).
- **No transfers to a person.** The agent takes a message instead. See [Transfers](/your-agent/transfers/).

Both switch on the moment you subscribe.

## Subscribing during the trial

You can subscribe at any point from **Billing**. Your card isn't charged until the trial ends; the plan is scheduled to start on that date, and the Billing page shows it as pending with the start date. You'll get an email a few days before saying the plan activates automatically and no action is needed.

If you'd rather start the paid plan immediately, for example to switch on transfers today, choose that option at checkout.

## Three days before the end

ViFi emails you that the trial is ending. If you've already subscribed, the email confirms the activation date instead.

## If the trial ends without a subscription

- You can still sign in, read calls, and change settings.
- Callers to your ViFi number hear a short message that the assistant is temporarily unavailable.
- Uploads to the knowledge base, new phone numbers, and texts pause.

Subscribe from **Billing** and everything resumes within a minute. Your number and settings are kept.

## Promo codes

The checkout has an **Add promotion code** field. Enter a code there before paying. See [Invoices and payment](/billing/invoices-and-payment/).
