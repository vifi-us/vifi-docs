Open **Billing** in the sidebar. You need the Admin role.

## Upgrading

1. Click the plan you want.
2. Review the **usage impact this billing period**: ViFi shows how your current usage fits the new plan.
3. Confirm. The change is immediate and the difference in price is prorated for the rest of the period.

## Downgrading

Self-serve downgrades are being re-enabled and will take effect at the end of the current period. <span class="status-pill status-pill--coming-soon">Coming soon</span>

For now, email [support@vifi.us](mailto:support@vifi.us) with the plan you'd like. Support will schedule it for the end of your period so you keep what you've paid for.

## Cancelling

1. Click **Manage billing** to open the billing portal.
2. Choose **Cancel subscription**.
3. Service continues until the end of the period you've paid for, then stops. Callers to your ViFi number hear the temporarily-unavailable message after that.

Before you cancel, turn off call forwarding on your business line so calls ring your own phone again. See [Forward your calls](/start-here/forward-your-calls/).

## Coming back

Sign in and subscribe again from **Billing**. Your settings, knowledge base, and call history are kept. Your phone number is kept for a period after cancellation; email support if you need to confirm it's still yours.

## Deleting your workspace and data

Cancelling doesn't delete data. To have the workspace and its calls, recordings, and caller profiles deleted, email [support@vifi.us](mailto:support@vifi.us) from an Admin's address. See [Data and privacy](/reference/data-and-privacy/).
