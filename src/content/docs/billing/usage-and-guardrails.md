Open **Billing** in the sidebar. Admins can see and change everything here.

## Usage this period

The usage meters show AI minutes and text segments used against what your plan includes, and how much extra usage has cost so far this period. Usage updates within minutes of each call.

::screenshot[The Billing page with the usage meters and the guardrails card]

At 100% of included minutes, ViFi emails you and extra minutes start being charged at your plan's rate. Your agent keeps answering; see below to change that.

## Guardrails

The **Billing guardrails** card has two amounts, both in dollars of *extra* usage per period:

- **Alert threshold.** When extra charges reach this amount, ViFi emails you. Nothing else changes.
- **Stop-limit.** When extra charges reach this amount, ViFi stops taking calls that would cost more until the next period, or until you raise the limit. Callers hear the temporarily-unavailable message.

Set the alert well below the stop-limit so you have time to react. Leave the stop-limit empty if you'd rather never miss a call.

## Text message quota

Text segments have their own meter. When you're close to the included amount, a banner appears on the Billing page. When texts run out, the agent's follow-up text is skipped rather than charged, unless your plan bills extra segments.

## Where the minutes went

Open **Calls** and sort by duration to find long calls. Common causes and fixes:

- Long greetings and fallback lines: shorten them. See [Greeting and personality](/your-agent/greeting-and-personality/).
- Callers left on hold during transfers: use transfer straight through. See [Transfers](/your-agent/transfers/).
- Spam calls: turn on automatic blocking. See [Blocked callers](/calls-and-callers/blocked-callers/).
- A high silence timeout: lower it. See [Call behavior](/your-agent/call-behavior/).
