ViFi uses Stripe for payment. Your card details are entered on Stripe's secure page and are never stored by ViFi.

## Paying

1. Open **Billing** and choose a plan.
2. On the checkout page, enter your card. If you have a promo code, click **Add promotion code** and enter it before paying.
3. Confirm. The monthly fee is charged at the start of each period; extra usage is charged at the end of the period it happened in.

## Updating your card

Click **Manage billing** on the Billing page. Stripe's billing portal opens, where you can change the card, update the billing address, and see upcoming charges.

## Invoices

The **Invoice history** card lists every invoice. Click **View invoice** to open it or **Download PDF** to save it. Invoices show the plan fee and any extra usage separately.

## When a payment fails

1. ViFi emails you (and texts you, if you've turned that on) that the payment failed.
2. Stripe retries the card over the next several days. Update the card in the billing portal to fix it sooner.
3. During this time your agent keeps answering, and the dashboard shows **Billing needs attention**.
4. If the retries all fail, the subscription is paused: you keep dashboard access, but callers hear the temporarily-unavailable message until a payment succeeds.

## Receipts for your accountant

Invoices include ViFi's business details and your billing address. If you need something added to them, such as a company registration number, set it in the billing portal under billing information.

## Questions about a charge

Email [support@vifi.us](mailto:support@vifi.us) with the invoice number.
