Customer-visible changes, newest first. For what's planned, see [What's coming](/reference/whats-coming/).

## September 2026

**Build your knowledge base from the files you already have** (6 September)

- **More file types.** Upload Word, PowerPoint, Excel, CSV, RTF, and JSON files as well as PDF, text, Markdown, and HTML. Long documents are read in full instead of the first dozen pages.
- **ViFi writes the articles.** After a document or web page is indexed, ViFi reads it and writes the questions and answers a caller might ask, tagged with where they came from. Edit one to keep your own version. See [Knowledge base](/your-agent/knowledge-base/).
- **Pages stay current.** Web pages are re-checked automatically and their answers rewritten when the page changes. Each workspace can add up to 50 pages and 200 documents.
- **Scans and photos.** A scanned PDF or a photo of a menu board is read with OCR and treated like any other document.
- **Add a whole site.** Enter your website and pick the pages to add from its sitemap in one step.
- **Import and export articles** as a spreadsheet from the Articles tab.
- **Sharper answers.** The agent no longer receives loosely related passages for off-topic questions, prefers a direct article over a passage, and knows which file or page a passage came from.

**Caller safety and setup polish** (5 September)

- **Blocked callers.** Block numbers by hand, or let ViFi block likely spam and rapid repeat callers automatically. Blocked calls are rejected before they're answered and don't use minutes. See [Blocked callers](/calls-and-callers/blocked-callers/).
- **Recording on by default** for new workspaces, with a retention period and an editable recording notice. See [Recording and compliance](/your-agent/recording-and-compliance/).
- **Pick your number.** The Setup Guide now shows available numbers for your area code and lets you choose. See [The Setup Guide](/start-here/setup-guide/).
- **Ten languages**, and the agent can switch to the caller's language mid-call. See [Voice and language](/your-agent/voice-and-language/).
- **Greeting preview.** Hear how the opening line will sound before saving.
- **Approved callers** are easier to find, and the Setup Guide is shown only to Admins.

## July 2026

**Better answers from documents** (29 July)

- Uploaded documents are split into sections so the agent finds the right passage instead of skimming the whole file. Long price lists and FAQs answer much more reliably. See [Knowledge base](/your-agent/knowledge-base/).

**Subscribe during the trial, promo codes** (29 July)

- Subscribe at any point in the trial and the plan starts automatically when the trial ends, with an email confirming the date. See [Your free trial](/billing/free-trial/).
- Enter a promotion code at checkout. See [Invoices and payment](/billing/invoices-and-payment/).

**Onboarding defaults**

- New workspaces start with sensible capabilities switched on, your own phone approved for test calls, and your website read into the knowledge base, so the first call works without configuration.
