## The short version

US carriers require any business that texts customers from a regular ten-digit number to be registered, with an approved description of what it sends and how customers agree to it. Messages outside the approved program are filtered or blocked, and violations can get a number shut off.

ViFi is registered as a single program that covers every business on the platform. That keeps you out of the paperwork, but it also means every text has to fit what's approved.

## What's approved today

One message: a one-time confirmation that the caller's request for a follow-up has been recorded, including the required HELP and STOP wording. The agent may only send it after reading the caller a short notice and hearing a clear yes.

That's why, today:

- The agent can't compose its own texts or send links.
- You can't reply to texts from the dashboard.
- Missed-call texts to callers are switched off.

See [Messages](/calls-and-callers/messages/) for the current state, and [Feature status](/reference/feature-status/) for what's planned.

## What's being added

A second program covering richer messages, such as links and ongoing conversations with callers who've agreed to them, is in progress with the carriers. <span class="status-pill status-pill--coming-soon">Coming soon</span>

## STOP, START, and HELP

Callers who text STOP to your number are opted out across ViFi and won't receive further texts from it. START opts back in. HELP returns a short help message. These are handled automatically and can't be changed.

## Texts to you

Texts ViFi sends *you*, such as sign-in codes and call summaries, come from ViFi's own toll-free number under a separate, verified program. You agree to them when you verify your mobile number, and STOP works the same way.

## Counting

Every segment sent or received on your ViFi number counts toward your plan's included segments. See [Plans and what's included](/billing/plans-and-pricing/).
