This list is drawn from work that's scheduled, not from ideas. Dates aren't promised. Items move to [What's new](/whats-new/) when they ship.

## Messaging

- **Richer texts to callers**: links, ongoing conversations, replies from the dashboard, and missed-call texts. Waiting on carrier approval of an expanded program. See [Text message rules](/reference/text-message-rules/).

## Your agent

- **Interruptions that work reliably.** The Allow interruptions switch is being reworked so it does what it says.
- **Better judgment about when to stop.** The agent staying on task when a caller still needs help, instead of taking a message and saying goodbye.

## Integrations

- **MCP servers**: connect your own systems to the agent. See [MCP servers](/connect-your-tools/mcp-servers/).
- **Automation analytics**: which automations fire, how often, and with what result.
- **Conditional automations**: run an automation only when a condition is met, such as negative sentiment.

## Account and billing

- **Sign in with Apple and Microsoft.**
- **Passkeys.**
- **Self-serve downgrades**, taking effect at the end of the period.
- **Data export** for your records.
- **Pending invitations** visible and cancellable on the Team page.

## Analytics

- **First-call resolution** and more agent-performance measures.
