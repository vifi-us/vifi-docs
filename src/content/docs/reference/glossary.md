title: Glossary
description: The words ViFi uses, in plain English.
sidebar:
  order: 2
---

**Action item.** Something your team needs to do after a call, pulled out of the conversation by ViFi. Shown on the call and on the caller's profile.

**Agent.** The AI assistant that answers your calls. You name it, pick its voice, and tell it how to behave.

**AI minute.** One minute of the agent's time on a call, rounded up per call. Plans include a number of them per month.

**Approved callers.** During the free trial, the up-to-three phone numbers that can reach your agent.

**Article.** A question-and-answer pair in the knowledge base.

**Automation.** Something ViFi does with a connected tool before, during, or after a call, such as posting a summary to Slack.

**Briefing.** Everything ViFi produces about a call: summary, action items, transcript, and recording.

**Caller facts.** Details a caller mentioned that ViFi remembers for next time, such as an address or a preference.

**Capability.** Something the agent is allowed to do on a call, such as take a message or book an appointment. Each has an on-off switch.

**Channel.** A way customers reach your agent: a phone number or the website widget.

**Disclaimer.** The recording notice read at the start of a call.

**Fallback line.** What the agent says when it can't answer and has nothing to look up.

**Forwarding.** Sending calls from your existing business number to your ViFi number, using a code you dial or a setting in your phone system.

**Integration.** A connection to another service, such as HubSpot or Google Calendar. Connect once; then choose automations.

**Knowledge base.** Articles, documents, and web pages the agent can search for answers.

**Overflow forwarding.** Forwarding only when your line is busy or unanswered.

**Policy.** A plain-English rule the agent follows on every call.

**Segment.** The unit carriers use to count text messages, about 160 characters each.

**Sentiment.** ViFi's read of how the caller felt: positive, neutral, negative, or mixed.

**Stop-limit.** A dollar amount of extra usage at which ViFi stops taking calls for the rest of the period.

**Transfer.** Handing a live call to a real person's phone.

**Trial.** The seven-day free period that starts when you pick a phone number.

**Unanswered.** The knowledge base tab listing questions callers asked that the agent couldn't answer.

**Webhook.** A way to send call data to another system by web address.

**Workspace.** One business's set-up in ViFi: its agent, calls, team, and billing. You can belong to several.
