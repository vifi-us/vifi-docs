This page summarises how the product handles data. The [Privacy Policy](https://vifi.us/legal/privacy) and [Terms of Service](https://vifi.us/legal/terms) are the binding documents.

## What's stored for each call

- The caller's phone number and the time, duration, and outcome of the call.
- The transcript, summary, action items, tags, and caller facts.
- The recording, if recording is on, until your retention period ends.
- Messages sent and received by text on your ViFi number.

## Caller profiles

ViFi builds a profile per phone number from what callers say on calls. Your team can edit or remove facts, and you can delete a profile on request. See [Callers](/calls-and-callers/callers/).

## Who can see what

- Your team, according to their [roles](/team-and-account/team-and-roles/). Recording playback is limited to roles with recording access and is logged.
- ViFi staff, only when needed to support you or keep the service running, and under confidentiality obligations.
- Connected tools receive what the automation you enabled sends them, such as a summary posted to Slack.

## How long things are kept

| Data | Kept |
|---|---|
| Recordings | Your retention setting, 90 days by default |
| Transcripts, summaries, caller profiles | For the life of the workspace |
| Invoices | For the life of the account and as required by law |
| Audit log | For the life of the workspace |

## Deleting data

- **A recording:** delete it from the call page. Logged.
- **A caller's data:** email [support@vifi.us](mailto:support@vifi.us) with the phone number.
- **A workspace and everything in it:** an Admin emails support.
- **Your own account:** from **Account**, or via support.

## Exporting data

A self-serve export of your workspace's data is planned. <span class="status-pill status-pill--coming-soon">Coming soon</span> Until then, email support and we'll prepare one.

## Where data lives

ViFi runs on cloud infrastructure in the United States. Calls are processed by speech and language providers under contract to ViFi; see the Privacy Policy for the current list.

## Security basics

Encryption in transit and at rest, two-factor authentication for your account, sign-in alerts, and per-role access. See [Security and sign-in](/team-and-account/security/).
