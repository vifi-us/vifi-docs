Open **Agent** in the sidebar and find the **Voice** section.

## Choosing a voice

1. Open the voice list. The **Recommended** group at the top is a good place to start.
2. Press the play button next to a voice to hear a sample.
3. Pick one and save.

The voice applies to every call. Callers hear the same voice whether the agent is greeting them, reading back a message, or transferring them.

::screenshot[The Voice section with the voice list open and a play button next to each voice]

:::note[Known issue]
Occasionally the voice list shows blank after you navigate away and come back, even though your voice is saved. Refresh the page. Your selection isn't lost.
:::

## Language

The **Language** setting is the language the agent speaks by default. It also affects the greeting: pick Spanish and the agent expects a Spanish greeting.

Supported languages: English, Dutch, French, German, Hindi, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese, Russian, and Spanish.

Choose a voice that suits the language. Voices are tagged with the languages they handle well, and the list prefers those matching your language setting.

## Switch to the caller's language

Turn on **Switch to the caller's language** and the agent listens for callers who answer in one of the supported languages. When it hears one, it understands them and replies in that language for the rest of the call. Your profile, knowledge base, and policies still apply; the agent translates as it goes.

Leave it off if you'd rather the agent always stay in one language.

## Names and words the voice mispronounces

If the agent says your business name or a product wrong, don't change the spelling in your profile. Add the word to the [pronunciation guide](/your-agent/pronunciation/) instead.

## For technical users: providers

**Settings**, then **Providers** shows which services handle speech-to-text, the language model, and text-to-speech for your workspace, and lets you pick the voice from the same catalog. You don't need to change anything there for normal use.
