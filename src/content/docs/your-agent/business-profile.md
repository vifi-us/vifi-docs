Your business profile is the first thing the agent reads before every call. Most of it is filled in during the Setup Guide from your website and Google listing. You can change any of it at **Settings**, then **Profile**.

::screenshot[Settings, Profile: business name, description, services and the hours grid]

## What's in it

| Field | What the agent does with it |
|---|---|
| **Workspace name** | Only shown to you, in the workspace switcher. Callers never hear it. |
| **Business name** | How the agent refers to your business on calls and in the greeting. |
| **Business description** | The agent's understanding of what you do. Two or three sentences in plain English work best. |
| **Services offered** | A list the agent can name when callers ask "do you do X?". Add one per line, for example "Pizza delivery", "Catering", "Dine-in". |
| **Industry** | Helps the agent pick the right vocabulary. Set on the **Agent** page under **Personality**. |
| **Business hours** | Powers answers to "are you open?" and "what time do you close on Saturday?". |

## Setting business hours

1. Go to **Settings**, then **Profile**, and find **Business Hours**.
2. For each day, switch between **Open** and **Closed**.
3. For open days, set the opening and closing time.
4. Save.

The Setup Guide sometimes leaves every day set to closed when it couldn't read your hours. The dashboard warns you about this. Fix it here; otherwise the agent tells callers you're closed.

:::tip
Hours only affect what the agent *says*. The agent answers calls at any hour. To change what happens after hours, see [After-hours and overflow](/best-practices/after-hours-and-overflow/).
:::

## Re-reading your website

ViFi reads your website and Google listing once, during the Setup Guide. It doesn't re-read them automatically. If your services or hours change, update the profile here, or add the new details as a [knowledge base](/your-agent/knowledge-base/) article.

## Writing a description the agent can use

Say what you do, who you do it for, and what makes you different, in the words a customer would use. Skip slogans. Compare:

- "Premium solutions for all your comfort needs." The agent can't do anything with this.
- "We repair and install air conditioning and furnaces for homes in the Atlanta area. Same-day service on most repairs. We don't service commercial buildings." The agent can now answer three common questions.
