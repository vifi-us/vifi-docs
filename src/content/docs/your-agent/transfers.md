When a caller asks for a person, has a complaint, or matches one of your routing rules, the agent can transfer the call to a phone number you choose.

Open **Agent** in the sidebar, find **Tools and capabilities**, and turn on **Transfer to a human**.

:::caution[Not available during the trial]
Transfers switch on once you subscribe. During the free trial the switch is locked and the agent takes a message instead. See [Your free trial](/billing/free-trial/).
:::

## Transfer destinations

Add one or more destinations under **Where to send callers, in order**. Each has:

- **A label**, such as "Front desk" or "Billing team".
- **A phone number**, in full with area code.
- **When to route here**, a short description the agent uses to decide, such as "Billing and invoice questions" or "Anyone asking for Maria".

The agent tries destinations in order. If the first doesn't answer, it tries the next. If nobody answers, it takes a message.

::screenshot[Two transfer destinations with their labels, numbers and routing descriptions]

## How the call is handed over

- **Announce the call first.** The agent calls the destination, tells the person who's calling and why, and then connects them. The caller waits on the line. Best when the person answering wants context.
- **Transfer straight through.** The caller is connected as soon as the destination answers, with no announcement. Faster, but the person answering starts cold.

## What callers hear

The agent tells the caller it's connecting them and stays quiet while the destination rings. Callers don't hear the announcement in the first mode.

## Tips

- Use your own mobile as the first destination while you're getting started. Once you trust the agent, move it further down.
- Give each destination a specific routing description. "General questions" on every destination gives the agent nothing to choose with.
- Transfers count as AI minutes while the agent is on the line, then the call continues on your phone. See [Usage and guardrails](/billing/usage-and-guardrails/).
