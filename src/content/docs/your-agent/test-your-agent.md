There are two ways to hear your agent without a real customer on the line.

## From the browser

1. Open **Agent** in the sidebar and find **Test Your Agent**.
2. Click to start. Allow microphone access when the browser asks.
3. Talk to the agent exactly as a caller would. Hang up when you're done.

The call shows up in **Calls** like any other, with a summary and transcript, so you can see what the agent understood.

::screenshot[The Test Your Agent panel with the call button and live transcript]

Before your trial starts, a small number of browser test calls are free and don't count against anything. After that, test calls use AI minutes like any other call.

## From your phone

Call your ViFi number from a phone on the [approved callers](/calls-and-callers/approved-callers/) list. After the trial, any phone works. This is the most realistic test because it goes through the same route as a customer's call.

## What to check

- Does the greeting sound natural at phone speed?
- Ask your three most common questions. Does it answer from your profile and knowledge base, or take a message?
- Ask something it shouldn't answer, such as a price you've told it not to quote. Does it follow the policy?
- Ask for a person. Does it transfer, or take a message, as you intended?
- Say goodbye. Does it wrap up rather than keep talking?

Write down what went wrong and fix it in the matching section: [Greeting](/your-agent/greeting-and-personality/), [Knowledge base](/your-agent/knowledge-base/), [Policies](/your-agent/call-behavior/), or [Transfers](/your-agent/transfers/). Then test again.
