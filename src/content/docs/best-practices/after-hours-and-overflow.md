ViFi answers whenever a call reaches it. *When* calls reach it is up to your forwarding. Here are the set-ups we see most.

## "Answer what we miss"

Forward when busy or unanswered. Your team picks up when they can; ViFi handles the rest. Set your carrier's ring delay to about four rings so callers don't wait too long.

Good defaults: transfers off during the day (you're already busy), messages on, summaries texted to the manager.

## "ViFi handles nights and weekends"

Forward everything at closing time and turn it off in the morning. Some phone systems can schedule this; otherwise it's a dial code twice a day.

Good defaults: a policy such as "We're closed now. Take a message and say someone will call back after 8am. For emergencies, transfer to the on-call number." Put the on-call number as the transfer destination with the routing description "Emergencies only".

## "ViFi is the front desk"

Give out the ViFi number and let the agent handle everything, transferring to people by department. Best with a full knowledge base and a connected calendar.

Good defaults: transfer destinations per department with clear routing descriptions, recording on with the disclaimer, daily summary email to the owner.

## "ViFi for one campaign"

Use the ViFi number on ads or a landing page only. Everything else stays as it was.

Good defaults: a policy describing the offer, a calendar connected for booking, HubSpot sync on so leads land in the CRM.

## Whichever you choose

- Tell the agent the plan in a policy. "You're answering after hours" changes how it handles "can I speak to someone?".
- Set business hours so the agent can tell callers when a person will be available.
- Watch [Analytics](/calls-and-callers/analytics/) for the missed-call count; it tells you if forwarding kicks in too late.
