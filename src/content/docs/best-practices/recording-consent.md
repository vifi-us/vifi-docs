## The basic question

In the United States, some states allow a call to be recorded when one party consents (which can be you), and others require every party to consent. Because a caller can be in any state, many businesses simply tell every caller at the start of the call. That's what ViFi's recording disclaimer is for.

## What ViFi does for you

- **Recording is on by default** for new workspaces, with the disclaimer switch available next to it.
- **The disclaimer is read at the start of the call** wherever you put the `{recording_disclaimer}` chip in your greeting. Callers who don't want to be recorded can hang up before saying anything substantive.
- **Recordings are private** and deleted after your retention period. Playback is logged.

See [Recording and compliance](/your-agent/recording-and-compliance/) for the settings.

## Questions to settle

- Do you need recordings at all? Transcripts and summaries are kept regardless and are enough for most businesses. Turning recording off removes the question entirely.
- How long should you keep them? Shorter is simpler. Ninety days is the default.
- Who on your team should be able to listen? Use roles to limit it. See [Team and roles](/team-and-account/team-and-roles/).
- Do you take payment details over the phone? If so, consider turning recording off or telling callers not to read card numbers to the agent.

## If a caller objects

They can ask you to delete a recording. Open the call and delete it from the recording player; the deletion is logged.
