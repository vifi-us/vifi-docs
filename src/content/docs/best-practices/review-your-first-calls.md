## Once a day for the first week

1. Open **Calls** and read every call from the day. Most take thirty seconds.
2. For each, ask three questions:
   - Did the agent understand what the caller wanted? If not, the summary will show it. Check the [business profile](/your-agent/business-profile/) covers that kind of request.
   - Did it have the answer? If it took a message when it could have answered, add an [article](/your-agent/knowledge-base/).
   - Did it end well? A caller who repeats themselves or asks for a person twice is a sign the fallback line or a policy needs work.
3. Open **Knowledge Base**, then **Unanswered**, and convert anything real.

## Once a week after that

- Sort calls by sentiment and read the negative ones. They're where the agent needs help.
- Skim **Unanswered** again.
- Check [Analytics](/calls-and-callers/analytics/) for the missed-call count and average duration.
- Ask one colleague to call the agent with a question you haven't tested.

## Signs you're done tuning

- Most calls end with a booking, an answered question, or a message that's actually useful.
- **Unanswered** is empty or only has questions you don't want answered.
- You've stopped reading every transcript.

At that point, switch to the daily summary email and check in weekly.
